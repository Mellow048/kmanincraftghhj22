# Narration — My Dad Has Died Four Hundred Times

**Status: scripted. One short, one story, no part two.**

Nine clips, ~44 shots, ~250 words, ~62 s. Built to `Topics/Stories/REFERENCE-STUDY.md`: impossible fact first,
the rule explained before the joke is spent, a debt nobody says out loud, a tactile payoff, ask last.

**Premise, three sentences.** My dad has died four hundred and eleven times in our world. Dying is free — you
wake up in your bed — so he kept walking into the same lava to pick up a music disc I dropped when I was eight.
The disc came back with him every single time, and so did he.

**The rule.** Death is free and respawn is at your bed. Break the bed and a death costs you the walk back from
world spawn.

| Planted | Pays off |
|---|---|
| "Dying is free" (1.free) | He spends deaths instead of anything real (4.body) |
| The disc comes back to the bed with him (4.almost) | He was never doing it for the disc: he was doing it for her (5.answer) |
| I counted the deaths for him (2.count) | Nobody else was counting, so nobody could tell him to stop (9.but) |
| The bed is what makes it possible (2.respawn) | I break it (6.break), and he still goes down (7.walked) |

```narration
# clip 01-hook
[1.hook]      My dad has died four hundred times in this game.
[1.notbad]    He's not bad at it.

# clip 02-rule
[2.respawn]   Every time, he wakes up in the same bed.
[2.walks]     Then he walks back down the same tunnel.
[2.count]     I counted the deaths for him.

# clip 03-why
[3.disc]      When I was eight, I dropped a music disc in the lava.

# clip 04-cost

[4.item]      You can't reach it. You can't swim in it.

[4.almost]    And the disc comes back to the bed with him. Every time.
```
