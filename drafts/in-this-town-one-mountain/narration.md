## 1. The film in four sentences

In this town we live at the bottom of the mountain, and we fish, and we save, because there is a gate at the top with a
price on it that goes up every year. The rich never think about us at all. Then my mum got sick and the one thing that
fixes her is one golden apple, eight gold, and there is no gold at the bottom of this mountain. So I climbed nine
thousand steps with the town's eleven years in my bag — and the bottom saved her, not the top.

**Logline:** a boy fishes the mountain's bottom flat to buy his sick mum the cure they cannot reach, the gate takes the
money and refuses the apple, and he comes home to find the whole town has traded a year of fish for it.

**Problem → tries → solution → truth**
- **Problem:** the cure is one golden apple, eight gold. The bottom has water and fish, not gold.
- **Tries:** fish the mountain stream for gold (catches two boots); fish all night; fish in the rain, then the storm;
  sell his rod; sell the boat. Nine days, forty-one emeralds.
- **Attempt:** climb nine thousand steps with the town's jar — the price has gone up again, and the gate will not sell
  an apple for emeralds at all.
- **Solution (not his):** the town fishes all night and pays **a year of every fish they catch** for one apple — the
  only currency the bottom has.
- **Truth:** the rich did not do it because she was sick. They did it because a year of fish is worth more than one
  apple. *"The top did not save my mum. The bottom did."*

**The fact the whole film turns on:** a golden apple costs eight gold, and fishing is how a poor player lives.

## 6. Three decisions I made that he has not said yes to

These are the only places where I chose. Everything else is his outline. Say no to any of them and I rewrite that part
and nothing else.

1. **The town pays in fish, not a big catch.** He said "try to get more money fast." I tried the other version: the boy
   lands one huge fish and sells it, or finds something in the water. It makes the ending his win — and then the line
   "the rich doesn't care" has nowhere to land, because the rich were never asked. Trading a year of fish instead keeps
   the rich rich, keeps the bottom the reason she lives, and makes the turn a sum the viewer can do. That is the film.
2. **The mum lives.** "No cure" in his outline is the pressure, not the ending. She eats the apple at midnight and is
   breathing by morning. If she dies, the film becomes a sad film, and sad ones are the set that sat at 1.5k–2k views.
3. **Nine thousand steps, not a bubble tube.** The hits used the game's own furniture (a ladder, a gate, a price). A tube
   to the top would be a bigger joke but it would make climbing cheap, and the climb is the only time the boy is alone
   with the jar.

One more, smaller: **the price never gets paid in emeralds by anyone.** The two families who did pay went up six years
ago and still wave — which is how the viewer learns the top is not a paradise before the boy does.

## 9. The honest risk

His two films that broke 20k were **1:40 and 1:45**. The 2:55 one had 82% stayed-watching and did not break out. So
2:45 is allowed, but it is the top of the range, not the middle of it — and the reason it survives here is that every
beat changes what the viewer believes:

- 0:00–0:25 we are poor and saving to go up (the dream)
- 0:25–0:45 the man in the clean coat, the town standing straight (the power)
- 0:45–1:20 she is sick, eight gold, boots in a stream (the problem, the joke, the fail)
- 1:20–1:50 the town votes, nine thousand steps, the gate says no (the climb and the shut door)
- 1:50–2:20 a street, just a street; the town in the rain; a year of fish (the turn)
- 2:20–2:45 she breathes, the jar stays full, the label comes off, subscribe

If it under-performs, the first thing to change is length, not story: cut `05-adults` and `12-friend` and it is 2:20.
