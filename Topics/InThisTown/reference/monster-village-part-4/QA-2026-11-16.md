# Part 4 — frame audit, 2026-11-16

40 frames of the **delivered** cut, one every 4 seconds, rendered by
`.github/workflows/stills.yml` on GitHub's machines (`commit_review: true` puts
the PNGs in `review/<composition>/` on a branch; that folder must be deleted
before delivery). This is the first time anybody has looked at the film as a
contact sheet rather than one still at a time.

## What the audit is for

`npm run check` proves the contract: cue order, camera moves, note lengths,
audio levels. It cannot see a frame. `Topics/InThisTown/OWNER.md` already says
so — *"Is the subject in the frame" is not a review: ask of every still, would
he know what this is with no sound and no line.* A contact sheet makes that
question cheap enough to ask 40 times.

## Found and fixed

**`4.tower` (38.0–40.3 s) — fixed.** `WatchTower` walls every storey below
6.7 m, and the old camera climbed from y=2.0 to y=9.6 at 6 m out, so the first
half of the shot was inside solid blocks. On the word **"tower"** the frame was
a screenful of brick and timber with no tower in it. Now the tower is shot full
height from the yard, low, with the deck, flag, ladders and lit floors readable
and the sleeper on the deck. Three framings were tried and rendered; the first
two failed (inside the walls; then the sleeper hidden behind the flagpole,
which stands at the deck's centre, same x/z as he sits).

## Found, not fixed — for a future pass

- **`9.slam` (150–153 s).** The note says the ravager "takes the golem in the
  ribs … the two of them go across the picture and through the front of a
  stone hut". At 150 s the frame is a lantern post, crates and an empty hut:
  the moment the line describes is the one moment neither of them is in the
  picture. The hut cannot break (the world is static), so the shot hides the
  hit behind `Boom`. Worth a cut on the impact.
- **`7.skel` (114 s) — "IT" frame.** On the word "it", a helmet on a stick
  drops toward a player whose face is out of frame; the subject reads as a
  bucket and a brown wall.
- **`6.earlier` (94 s).** On the marching half of the joke, dad and the heap of
  guards are behind a hut; only the enderman's legs are in shot.
- **Captions sit at `y: 0.72`**, 22 % up from the bottom — on the chest of any
  character framed close. At `6.where` (90 s) "that" lands on the boy's mouth;
  at `5.fell` (58 s) "me" lands on his chest; at `7.horde`/`4.gers` the words
  sit between the witches' hats. This is the house style in every film in this
  topic and not a part-4 defect, but it is a real cost on close-ups.

## Verdict on the 10–37 s remaster

Attempted three ways and reverted. The stretch is told from outside the cage
on purpose — the guard walks to the bush, and the jumpscare is aimed at the
viewer. Cutting back to the teller there means cutting to a boy **asleep**,
because he says so four times ("I could not sleep" is the guard, "I was
asleep", "I did not see any of that", and section 5 rests on him sleeping
through the fight). The only true cutback is a near-duplicate of `1.asleep`,
which plays 1.5 s earlier, framed worse through cage bars. Rendered frames of
each attempt are what settled it. **The narration is doing the work here, and
the narration cannot be touched.**
