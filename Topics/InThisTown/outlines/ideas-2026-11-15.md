# Ideas put to the owner, 2026-11-15 (after the Monster Village study)

He asked for ideas "so i get interested", built on his outlines, the reference ideas and Monster Village part 1 (about
5k views). All six follow the recipe in [`../reference/monster-village/README.md`](../reference/monster-village/README.md):
a mob every player knows as the family, the teller the odd one out, a rule that looks unkind and is love, and the
thing the viewer usually is arriving as the danger. Each turns on a true Minecraft fact.

**Status: he chose idea 5 ("lets do 5", 2026-11-16).** It is built as `in-this-town-everyone-runs-away-from-me`
(a whole story in one film, 1:40, 50 shots). The script is that film's `narration.md`. He picked it from the pitch
alone: the script was not shown to him before the build, so the first render is also the script review. **He approved that render
("really cute short, i like it", 2026-11-16) and asked for another.** **Idea 4 (the enderman mum) is built**: he approved the script on 2026-11-16 ("another banger idea! go ahead lad
make that mp4"), and the film is `src/videos/in-this-town-i-have-never-seen-my-mums-face/` (whole in one film, moved
from the End to an overworld town; render in `renders/`). His verdict on the finished film (2026-11-16): **"i like it but it was kinda similar to the creeper one … it
might get me in trouble of reused content"**. He is right: ideas 1, 4, 5 and 6 below are one outline with four mobs
(the odd kid, the mob mum and dad, the players who come). **So ideas 1 and 6 are parked. Idea 2 would need another
danger than players. Idea 3 still stands.** Do not pitch any of them as new. The next set, made to differ from the
two films built from this page, is [`ideas-2026-11-16.md`](ideas-2026-11-16.md).
