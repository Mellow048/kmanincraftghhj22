# Meme-short ideas, 2026-11-18 (the "HUS HUS HUS" engine)

The owner brought a reel he made on another agent — *The Guy Who Never Went To Parties* — and asked for the same thing,
not the same story. This page is the research, the engine, and three premises written to it.

## 1. What the meme actually is

"Hus hus hus" is not a word. It is a **rhythm** — a chant for running flat out, used in edits over someone sprinting,
chopping, or working at absurd speed. It carries two things at once:

- **urgency** (something is happening too fast to explain), and
- **nonsense** (it means nothing, so the brain files it as a sound effect, not a line of dialogue).

That is why it works as a *catchphrase*: it can open the film cold, it can punctuate the training montage, and it can
come back at the end as the punchline — and it never has to be explained. **The joke is never the syllable; the joke
is how calm the person is about the speed.**

The reel's other engine, which matters more than the sound:

| Beat | What it does |
|---|---|
| Cold open, mid-action, then a **rewind** | The film starts at the peak of the skill, then rewinds to explain it |
| The norm | Everybody in town does the fun thing; he does not |
| The invitation | Friends shout at him to join. He declines, politely, and we like him for it |
| The dream | One shot of what he wants to be, seen from a couch |
| First failure | Small, physical, funny. The floor is always the punchline |
| **Training montage** | Time passes in cuts; he gets *worse* before better; the catchphrase lives here |
| The job | He walks into the professional version of his hobby |
| The reunion | The friends see him do it, and cannot believe it |
| The answer | One word, deadpan: **"Training."** |
| The button | The catchphrase again, faster, and hard cut to black |

**Our twist, every time:** the sound is a Minecraft sound, the skill is a Minecraft skill, and one real game fact is
the reason he wins. No Roblox, no borrowed plot.

## 3. A. THE GUY WHO NEVER WENT TO THE NETHER — 1:45

### Beat 1 — Cold open (0:00–0:06)
**Picture:** extreme close-up of a boy's face, sweat, goggles, lantern light. He drops a pinch of nether wart into a
bubbling stand. Three bottles fill at once.
**Sound:** `BLUB BLUB BLUB. BLUB BLUB BLUB.` Then the last bubble lands with a *ding*.
**Then: rewind.** The whole frame whips backwards, bottles un-fill, the wart jumps back into his fingers.
**Narration:** none. The sound is the hook.

### Beat 2 — The norm (0:06–0:20)
**Picture:** a Nether portal in the middle of a village square, lit, roaring. Villagers and kids queue to jump in,
carrying swords they cannot use. Fireworks. A ghast screams somewhere far off; everybody cheers.
**Friend 1:** "BRO! THE NETHER! COME ON!"
**Friend 2:** "STOP BEING BORING! YOU ONLY LIVE ONCE!"
**Picture:** cut to him at home, alone, in front of a brewing stand, a book open on his knees.
**Narration:** "In this town, everybody loved the Nether. Every night, my friends went through the portal. But I had a
different dream."

### Beat 3 — The dream (0:20–0:30)
**Picture:** the town's castle kitchen. A royal alchemist in a white coat pours one potion, and forty guards stop
burning at once. The crowd carries him out on their shoulders.
**Narration:** "I wanted to be the royal alchemist. I wanted to make a potion so good that nobody in this town would
ever burn again."
**Picture:** he ties an apron, picks up a glass bottle, and walks into his kitchen like it is a battlefield.

### Beat 4 — The first failure (0:30–0:42)
**Picture:** he drops blaze powder into a bottle, screws the lid on, and shakes it. It goes *white*. He looks at the
camera. It explodes. His eyebrows are gone. The cat leaves the room.
**Him:** "ALMOST! ALMOST!"
**Picture:** the puddle on the floor, still smoking. He mops it with one hand, holding a bottle in the other.
**Narration:** "Well. I still had a lot to learn."

### Beat 5 — The training arc (0:42–1:12)
Montage, cut to the beat, months passing:

1. **Nether wart**, one at a time, into a stand. Too slow. Then faster. Then a blur.
   **Him:** "BLUB BLUB BLUB! BLUB BLUB BLUB!"
2. **Failure ladder**: a potion that turns him blue; a potion that makes him levitate into the ceiling and stay there
   for a full shot; a potion that turns his hair into a mushroom; a shelf of labelled disasters.
3. **Calibration**: he lines up forty bottles in a row and fills them to exactly the same height first try.
4. **The real thing**: a drop of his brew on his tongue, and he steps into a wall of fire for one second and comes out
   with his eyebrows back.
5. Calendar: pages flip past. Seasons change outside the window. The cat grows old and leaves again.

**Narration:** "While my friends were in the Nether, I was in that kitchen. I ruined four hundred potions. I used up
eleven blaze rods. I fell asleep standing up twice, and once on the ceiling."
**Narration:** "Months passed. And then one morning, the potion came out gold."

### Beat 6 — The job (1:12–1:28)
**Picture:** the castle. He walks in wearing a white coat that is too big, carrying a wooden box of bottles. Guards
sniff the box.
**Narration:** "One day, the castle hired me. They said the last alchemist had retired. He had also exploded."
**Picture:** the castle infirmary, forty guards groaning, all of them pink from the Nether. He sets down his box,
uncorks one bottle, and pours a single cup.
**Picture:** rapid cuts — a cup, a guard drinking, the burn fading off an arm; another guard; another; a whole ward
going quiet at once; the head alchemist's face, absolutely stunned.

### Beat 7 — The reunion (1:28–1:45)
**Picture:** his two friends stumble in through the door, still smoking, still in their Nether gear, one of them
holding a sword he clearly never used.
**Friend 1:** "WAIT. IS THAT HIM?!"
**Friend 2:** "BRO?! HOW DID YOU GET THAT GOOD?!"
**Picture:** he turns around slowly, holding a bottle.
**Him:** "Training."
**Beat of silence.** The bottles behind him bubble once.
**Picture:** crash zoom into his face, goggles down.
**Him:** "BLUB BLUB BLUB! BLUB BLUB BLUB!"
**Picture:** final rapid cuts — three stands bubbling at once, forty bottles filling, the counter flooding with gold
potions — **hard cut to black.**

**End card line (the ask, inside the story):** "Subscribe if you would have drunk the blue one."

## 4. Why this one travels

- The hook is a **sound and a face**, no explanation: exactly the reel's opening, and nothing like our other films.
- The norm is a thing every player does (the Nether, badly) and the skill is a thing almost nobody learns
  (**brewing**), so the joke is on the viewer without insulting them: *you went through the portal with a stone
  sword, didn't you.*
- The payoff is a **picture**: forty burnt guards, one cup, and the burn leaving an arm in a single shot.
- The one-word answer lands because the film has earned it with eleven blaze rods and four hundred ruined potions.
- The catchphrase is a Minecraft sound, so it is ours, and it can be reused in every short of this shape.
