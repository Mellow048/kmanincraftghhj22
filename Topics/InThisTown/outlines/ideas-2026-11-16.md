frame one is something every player knows on sight, doing the wrong thing; line one is "I"; the jokes and the turns
are true Minecraft facts; the viewer (a player) is in the story. **Brought back**, because the creeper and enderman

## 1. *In This Town, I Was Told to Sit*

> In this town, I was told to sit. That was four hundred days ago. I have not moved. I am a very good boy.

**Frame one:** a Minecraft dog in a red collar, sitting dead still in the middle of a busy town square, with a bird's
nest on his head and "DAY 400" over him. Villagers walk round him without looking.

**The story.** His owner, a player, said "sit" and walked off. So he sat. The base emptied. Grass grew over it.
Villagers came and built a town around him: the road bends round him, the well was dug beside him, kids hang their
hats on him, and the town thinks he is a statue. A skeleton walks past and he may not chase it (it runs anyway). A
creeper stands next to him all night. It rains. He does not move, because he was told to sit. He thinks he was
forgotten, and he has reasons: a bed beside him that nobody ever slept in, a chest of bones ("he forgot his lunch"),
torches all round him, a roof over just his spot. Then he says them back as a list, the bed, the bones, the torches,
the roof, and sees it: a dog who is not sitting follows you anywhere, even somewhere you will not come back from. He
was not left behind. He was kept safe. And the bed is where a player comes back when it goes wrong. The bed lights
up. His owner stands there with nothing: no sword, no armour, half a heart, in a town that was not there when he
left. He looks at the dog. The dog has not moved. "He said one word. And I stood up."

**Minecraft facts:** a sitting dog stays where it is for ever and never disappears; a dog that is not sitting follows
you and teleports to you; its tail shows how it feels; skeletons run from dogs; a player who dies comes back at his
bed with nothing.

**Why it could travel:** every player has left a dog sitting somewhere and feels bad about it. The question (does he
come back?) is asked in line one and answered in the last ten seconds. A day counter runs through the film.

**To build:** a real Minecraft dog (four legs, sitting, a tail that goes up and down, a collar): new and small. Not
the dog people from the cats and dogs film. The town that grows is pack parts. Whole in one film, about 1:45.

## 2. *In This Town, Being Scared Is My Job*

> In this town, being scared is my job. There is a zombie outside my window. He has been there for two years. His
> name is Kevin.

**Frame one:** a villager in a glass box high in the sky, arms up, screaming at a zombie who sits in a boat one block
away with a name tag: KEVIN. A clock on the wall. The scream stops; he checks the clock.

**The story.** It is an iron farm. Three villagers work in shifts. Every time they are scared enough, an iron golem
pops into being on the platform, takes one step, and the water carries it off. "We do not talk about where the
golems go." Iron clinks into a chest below, with one poppy. The player who owns the farm comes once a week, empties
the chest and never looks up. The trouble: after two years, he is not scared of Kevin any more. Kevin waves. They
play noughts and crosses on the glass. No fear, no golems, no iron. The owner finds an empty chest, and comes back
with a new zombie on a lead and a bucket of lava for the old one. To save Kevin, the villager has to be more scared
than he has ever been, on purpose, and for a friend: and the golem that appears is the first one that does not step
into the water. It looks at the glass. It looks at Kevin. "I said: he is with us." They walk to the edge of the
platform, and the farm is two hundred blocks up. Like and subscribe for part 2.

**Minecraft facts:** scared villagers make iron golems; farms are built in the sky with a zombie in a boat; a mob
with a name tag never disappears; a golem leaves iron and poppies; golems go for zombies.

**Why it could travel:** half the players watching have built this machine and have never thought about who is in it.
"His name is Kevin" is the fourth line. It ends on a cliffhanger, which he likes.

**To build:** the farm (glass cells, a water channel, a boat, a chest): medium. The villager, the zombie, the golem
and the player are in the pack.

## 3. *In This Town, a Stranger Moved Into My House*

> In this town, a stranger moved into my house. He did not knock. He slept in my bed. Then he put my bed in his
> pocket.

**Frame one:** a villager stands beside his own bed. A player is in it, asleep, with his boots on.

**The story.** A villager, flat and polite, lists what the stranger did, and every item is something every player
does. He took the bread out of my chest while looking me in the eye. He jumped on our wheat. He took the town bell
("we used it to know when to panic"). He dug a hole in the square and went straight down, and came up three days
later with a pickaxe that glowed. He put me in a boat. "I do not know why. I was in the boat for a week." He built a
wall round my room and put a desk in it: "that is how I became a librarian". He broke the desk and put it back
forty-one times, looking more and more disappointed, then shouted "MENDING" and gave me all his emeralds. In this
town we do not say anything; we say "hrmm". Then the raid comes, the town runs, and one person stands in the
gate: the stranger, ringing a bell. "It was our bell." He wins. Hero of the village. "We gave him a discount. He
still has my bed."

**Minecraft facts:** all of it: sleeping in villager beds and taking them, looting chests, trampling crops, taking
the bell, digging straight down, villagers in boats, the lectern broken again and again for a Mending book, the raid,
Hero of the Village and its discount.

**Why it could travel:** the viewer is the stranger. Each line is "I do that", which is a reason to comment and to
send it to a friend. It is the cheapest to make.

**To build:** nothing. Villagers, players, pillagers, the bell, beds and the village are in the pack. Whole in one
film, about 1:30.

## 4. *In This Town, I Have Forty Minutes*

> In this town, I have forty minutes. I sell things nobody wants. I have two llamas. And nobody has ever bought
> anything.

**Frame one:** the wandering trader, in his blue robe with two llamas, arms wide beside a little stall. Over his head
a clock: 40:00, counting down. A whole town walks past him.

**The story.** The clock stays on screen for the whole film. He sells one sapling, one dead bush, a fish in a bucket
and a slime ball, five emeralds each. The villagers will not buy: "it is a town of salesmen". A player stops, looks
at his goods, looks at his llamas, and takes the leads. At night he drinks a potion and goes invisible, "so nobody
has to feel bad about not buying". He has done this in nine hundred towns. With three minutes left a small kid comes
up with one emerald: he wants the dead bush, for his mum. It costs five. The trader looks at the clock, and sells it
for one. His first sale. He turns round to tell the llamas, the clock hits 0:00, and he is gone. The kid stands
there holding the bush. Then: another town, 40:00, and he is whistling. "Like and subscribe. I have thirty-nine
minutes."

**Minecraft facts:** the wandering trader turns up with two llamas, stays about forty minutes and vanishes; he
drinks an invisibility potion at night; he sells odd things for too much; players take his leads.

**Why it could travel:** everybody knows him and nobody has ever felt sorry for him. A clock counting down on screen
is a reason to stay to the end, and countdowns are something the owner likes.

**To build:** the trader (a villager in a blue hooded robe): small. A llama: new, medium.

## 5. *In This Town, We Lost the Vote* (the wild one)

> In this town, everybody lost a vote. I am a cow with flowers on. I lost to a squid.

**The story.** The town where the mobs that lost the Mob Vote live: the yellow cow with flowers, the one that throws
ice, the grumpy bush, the one that plays hide and seek in mines, the little stone golem, the crab, the penguin.
Every year three new ones stand on a stage; one becomes real, and two move in here. The copper golem lived here for
four years. Then one morning a letter came. He was added. The whole town walked him to the gate. "It can happen."

**Minecraft facts:** the real Mob Votes and who lost them; the copper golem lost in 2021 and was added in 2025.

**The risk:** only viewers who followed the votes will get it, and it needs six or seven new mobs: the biggest build
of the five. Good for comments from fans; weak in frame one for everybody else.

added.

## On "reused content"

The agent could not open YouTube's policy pages either. As far as it knows, "reused content" there is about using
other people's material, which these films do not: the script, the voice, the pictures and the sound are made here.
The rule nearer to his worry is the one about channels whose videos are so alike that a viewer can hardly tell them
apart. Two films are not that. A row of ten made from one outline could be, and long before any rule, a viewer who
thinks "I have seen this one" swipes away. So the fix is the one above, whatever the rule says.
