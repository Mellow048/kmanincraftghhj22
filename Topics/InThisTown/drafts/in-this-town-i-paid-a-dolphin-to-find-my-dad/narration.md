# In This Town, I Paid a Dolphin to Find My Dad

**Script for approval · short cut · one story, no Part 2.** The owner approved the [pitch](../../outlines/ideas-2026-11-18.md). This is the full script before recording or building. One kid telling the story afterward; Dad's quoted words are part of that telling, not a second voice.

Start on the dolphin coming out of the water for the fish. Let the matching stripe and Dad's hand register before the lines that name them. The fish in the first shot comes back as two fish in the last.

## Spoken script

```narration
# clip 01-the-payment
[1.pay] In this town, I paid a dolphin to find my dad.
[1.empty] The storm was over. Dad still hadn't come home.
[1.half] Only half of his oar had washed up.
[1.stripe] I knew that crooked stripe. I'd painted it.
[1.lunch] Dad used to feed this dolphin his lunch.
[1.guess] I hoped it knew which way he'd gone.

# clip 02-the-reef
[2.search] The search boats all headed out to sea.
[2.turn] The dolphin turned the other way. Toward the reef.
[2.tie] I tied my boat to an old wreck.
[2.dive] The dolphin went under. I went after it.

# clip 03-the-chest
[3.hope] I thought it had spotted Dad.
[3.chest] It had spotted a chest.
[3.gold] The lid was cracked. I could see gold inside.
[3.leave] I'd have stopped for that any other day.

# clip 04-the-stripe wait 0.14
[4.red] Then something red slid through a gap in the deck.
[4.match] The white stripe bent the same way as mine.
[4.hand] The oar moved again. Someone was holding it.
[4.dad] Then I heard him. “Is that you?”
[4.here] I stuck my hand through the gap. He grabbed it.

# clip 05-the-hatch
[5.reef] Dad told me his boat had hit the reef.
[5.cabin] He'd climbed into the part of the wreck above water.
[5.beam] But a beam had fallen across the hatch.
[5.pull] I pulled till my feet slipped. Nothing.
[5.push] Dad pushed from below. It didn't move.
[5.boat] Then I saw the half I'd left in my boat.
[5.under] I slid the oar under the end of the beam.
[5.now] “Now, Dad!” I leaned down as he pushed up.
[5.open] The beam rolled clear. The hatch came up.

# clip 06-the-way-home wait 0.20
[6.up] He reached out. This time I could grab his arm.
[6.boat] I held on till he was in the boat.
[6.chest] We left the gold where it was.
[6.home] Dad opened our fish box back at the dock.
[6.first] He held a fish out over the water.
[6.second] Then he reached in and took out another.
[6.double] Dad paid him double.
[6.close] Subscribe if you'd hire him.
```
