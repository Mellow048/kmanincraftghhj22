## How the agent read three things (tell him)

- **"the kid who outran you" / "the small kid who outpaced you"** is the baby villager from part 1. He comes in fifth,
  and later he is the one in the van with the ice cream.
- **The buttons**, as part 1 set them: first = light speed, second = "much too fast", third = "just right". The teller
  runs home on the first, then goes back to the third. In the desert he presses the second and holds it down to a
  jog ("too fast… just right"). First place's "BOOM" is his first button.
- **"to avoid the cameras"**: the race has camera drones over the runners from this part on.

## What the agent added

Almost nothing: "I was not getting in a van", and the van driver's mouth falling open for his "baffled".

## Script draft 1 (about 590 words, about 2:25)

In this town, the fastest kid wins a trophy. And I was the slowest kid in town.
Then a witch gave me a pair of shoes. They had three buttons. Light speed. Too fast. And just right.
I came second at the rivers. And the kid who came first was wearing the same shoes.
He turned round. I was gone. He looked confused.
I was behind a tree. I could not believe it. The same shoes!
Then I heard three more runners come in. Third. Fourth. Fifth.
Fifth was the baby. The one who used to beat me.
Then two more came in. They were friends of first place. I looked at their feet.
They were wearing those shoes too!
I got back behind the tree. I did not peek anymore.
Then I had an idea.
I pressed the first button. Light speed.
I ran from the rivers, all the way to my house.
I grabbed a paint brush. And I painted my shoes blue.
If they got found out, nobody would look at mine.
Then I ran back, at the speed of light. And I pressed the third button again.
First place saw me. "Hey. Where did you come from?"
I froze. I did not expect a question.
"I was pooping," I said.
Wow. What a great excuse.
"In the river?" he said. I nodded.
He thought it was weird. But he was first place. He did not think about it much.
He looked at my shoes. Blue. Then he went back to his friends.
I sighed. He did not suspect a thing.
Ten minutes later, everybody was back. We lined up again.
I drank a lot of water. I was ready.
Then I saw first place and his friends bend down. They pressed something on their shoes.
I got nervous.
Three. Two. One. Go!
All of us ran! First place and his friends took the lead.
I had forgotten to change mine. They were still on the third button.
I did not press it. Not yet. Those three might get caught.
So I blended in. I was ninth.
Then we ran into the desert. It was hot.
Everybody was exhausted. Me too. All the water I drank was gone.
Far in front, I could see the three of them. Still in the lead. But tired too.
Then a van drove up next to us. "You can get in, if you want to quit!"
That made me angry. All those years, I was the slowest one.
I was not getting in a van.
So I bent down in the crowd, away from the cameras. And I pressed a button.
I jogged. Too fast. I slowed down. Just right. A bit faster than before.
Then I ran. The van driver's mouth fell open.
I was catching up. First place and his friends looked back. They were shocked.
They looked at my feet. Blue shoes. His friends did not suspect a thing.
First place did. "Maybe he painted them blue," he said.
He got mad. He pressed his button. BOOM! He was gone.
His friends fell behind. I did not copy him. I stayed behind them, and I blended in.
Then I saw the van again. The baby was inside it. He had quit!
But he had an ice cream. I was jealous.
Seven minutes later, I was fourth.
His friends were second and third. And first place was still first place.
I was exhausted. I went to get a drink.
And while I was drinking, first place walked up to me.
