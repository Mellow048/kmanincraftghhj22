## Script draft 2 (697 words, about 2:50; the wall is 2:58, so a line or two may have to go once the voice is recorded)

In this town, I was the only one who was not a monster. And I was in a cage.
It was night. Half of the castle was asleep. The other half was on the night shift.
I was asleep too. So I did not see any of this. They told me later.
One guard woke up in his room. He could not sleep.
He looked at the ceiling. He counted sheep. It did not work.
So he went outside, for some fresh air.
It was a nice night. There were stars. He took a big breath.
Then a bush moved.
"Who is there?" he said. The bush did not say.
He pulled out his sword. He walked up to the bush. Slowly. Very slowly.
BAM! It was my dad. With his jaw wide open.
The guard screamed. It was a very long scream.
My dad was meant to be quiet. He put his jaw back. "Sorry," he said.
The whole castle heard it. A light went on in every window.
Up on the tower, one player was asleep on the job.
He woke up. He picked up his spyglass. He looked at the forest.
Trees. Trees. Trees. Then the trees moved.
Pillagers. Hundreds of them.
And vindicators. And evokers. And witches. And some I do not know the name of.
Then something small and blue filled the whole spyglass. A vex.
It flew right at his face.
That is when I woke up.
Behind me, somebody fell off a tower. I did not see that either.
Then the wall exploded. BOOM! The pillagers had brought TNT.
The villagers ran. Not away. They ran to the big gate. And they let it out.
The iron golem. It was as tall as a house.
It walked through the smoke. Every step shook my cage.
The pillagers were not scared. They were angry. They opened a gate of their own.
A ravager. With a pillager riding on top.
They ran at each other. The golem's fist. The ravager's jaw. The ground shook.
The ravager went back ten steps. Then it came again.
Then, pop. Somebody was standing at my cage. The enderman teacher.
He had a key. He was trying it in the lock. His hands were shaking.
"Where did you get that key?" I said.
"Do not ask," he said.
Four minutes earlier.
Then they came through the hole in the wall. My friends. All of them!
My mum. My dad. Hiss. The zombies. The skeletons. The spiders.
On the other side, the villagers and the players put on their armour. All of it.
It did not help.
A zombie took a sword, and bent it.
A skeleton shot a helmet off. Then the one under it.
A spider tied three players together.
My mum did not fight. She walked through all of them, looking for me. Nobody stopped her.
And Hiss only had to walk at them. They ran.
Then the bus arrived. It came over the wall. Splat.
Click. My cage was open.
"Get in!" said the teacher. I got in the bus. I was wet. I did not care.
Then I looked out through the slime. And I saw Mo.
He was down on the ground. He was not moving.
Two guards had him by the arms. They were dragging him away.
I jumped out of the bus.
Everybody turned to look at me. My mum. My dad. All of them.
And nobody was looking at the golem.
It ran right at the bus. One flick.
The bus went over the wall, and into the forest.
Then the golem turned to me. It lifted its fist.
SLAM! The ravager hit it from the side. They both went through a house.
I ran. And somebody ran after me. Small feet. Hiss.
"Stay back!" shouted the teacher. "Both of you!"
Hiss did not stay back. Hiss had never stayed back in his life.
The castle door was open. I went in.
It was a hallway. Long, and thin. So thin I could touch both walls.
At the far end, two guards. And Mo, on the floor between them.
I ran at them.
