# Narration — In This Town, Everyone Runs Away From Me

**Status: draft 1 (2026-11-15), built on the owner's pick without a script review. Voice `voice-03`, tempo 1.45.**

Topic: `Topics/Stories`. Short cut. **A whole story in one film**, like the cats and dogs: no cliffhanger, no
"part two".

**Where it came from.** The owner asked for ideas made to the Monster Village recipe
(`Topics/Stories/Old Reference/monster-village/README.md`, "Why this one travelled"). Six were put to him
(`Topics/Stories/outlines/ideas-2026-11-15.md`); he said **"lets do 5"**. The pitch he chose, word for word:

> "In this town, I am the only cat. Everyone else is a creeper. And every one of them runs when I say hello." A funny
> one, whole in one film like the cats and dogs. He just wants one hug. Players come to farm gunpowder and the
> creepers start to hiss. So he walks at them, and the whole town stampedes away from him, straight through the
> players. One small creeper stays, shaking, and lets him sit down.

**What the script adds to that pitch:** his creeper mum and dad who found him in a box; the nine-block dinner table
(six by the end); his own row and his own door at school; the three things he tries (a creeper costume, walking
backwards, sitting still); the purr that sends the little creeper off for a week; "you cannot hiss and run at the same
time"; the one shoe; and the ending is his mum's hug, a lean, because creepers have no arms. The little creeper of
the pitch sits by him in the last beat.

**The recipe, and where each step is:** frame one is a famous mob and a kid with one thing wrong (a cat kid waves,
a street of creepers runs); line one is "I"; mum, dad and where he came from by second 9; a rule that looks unkind
and is not (they love him "from the other side of the room"); the thing the viewer usually is arrives as the danger
(`4.night`, players); the small one stands up for his family (`5.behind`); the game's own facts are the jokes
(creepers run from cats, gunpowder comes from creepers, a hiss means a blast, creepers have no arms).

**One sentence:** The only cat in a town of creepers, who all run from him and who he only wants a hug from, saves
them from players by doing the one thing he is good at: saying hello.

**One teller, first person: a cat kid in a red shirt.** The town is told in the present (it is still there), the
night the players came in the past. Plain short words. No lesson.

```narration
# clip 01-hook
[1.cat]      In this town, I am the only cat.
[1.all]      Everyone else is a creeper.
[1.hello]    And every one of them runs when I say hello.
[1.mum]      My mum is a creeper. My dad is a creeper.
[1.box]      They found me in a box, as a kitten.
[1.love]     They love me very much. From the other side of the room.

# clip 02-life wait 0.3
[2.why]      Creepers are scared of cats.
[2.knows]    Nobody knows why. Not even the creepers.
[2.table]    Our dinner table is nine blocks long. I sit at the far end.
[2.school]   At school, I have my own row. And my own door.
[2.street]   When I walk down the street, it empties. Just like that.
[2.never]    I do not scratch. I do not bite. I have never even chased a chicken.
[2.hug]      All I ever wanted was one hug.

# clip 03-tries wait 0.3
[3.tried]    So I tried everything.
[3.suit]     I dressed up as a creeper.
[3.suitran]  They ran from the costume.
[3.back]     I walked backwards, so nobody could see my face.
[3.tail]     They ran from my tail.
[3.still]    Once, I sat still for a whole day.
[3.little]   And a little creeper sat down next to me.
[3.purr]     Then I purred. I could not help it.
[3.gone]     I did not see him for a week.

# clip 04-players wait 0.3
[4.night]    Then one night, players came.
[4.six]      Six of them. With swords, and empty sacks.
[4.powder]   They wanted gunpowder. And gunpowder comes from creepers.
[4.square]   My whole town backed into the square.
[4.started]  And they started to hiss.
[4.hiss]     When a creeper hisses, it is about to blow up.
[4.chain]    And when one blows up, they all do.
[4.white]    My mum was flashing white. My dad was flashing white.

# clip 05-hello wait 0.4
[5.small]    I am small. I cannot fight six players.
[5.good]     But there is one thing I am very, very good at.
[5.behind]   So I walked up behind my whole town.
[5.said]     And I said hello.
[5.ran]      They ran. Every single one. Straight ahead.
[5.through]  Straight through the players.
[5.boom]     Nobody blew up.
[5.cant]     You cannot hiss and run at the same time.
[5.left]     The players left their swords, their sacks, and one shoe.

# clip 06-hug wait 0.4
[6.morning]  In the morning, the whole town came back.
[6.nine]     They stopped in front of me. Nine blocks away.
[6.step]     Then my mum took one step. And one more.
[6.shake]    She was shaking all over.
[6.arms]     Creepers have no arms. So she leaned on me.
[6.second]   It lasted one whole second. Then she ran.
[6.came]     But she came back.

# clip 07-now wait 0.35
[7.turns]    Now I get one hug a day. They take turns.
[7.table]    The table is six blocks long.
[7.little]   And that little creeper sits next to me every day. He lets me purr.

# clip 08-sub wait 0.4
[8.sub]      Like and subscribe. Or I will come and say hello.
```
