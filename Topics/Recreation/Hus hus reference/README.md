## That’s Me. The Elbow.

**[Watch / download the approved MP4](https://github.com/Mellow048/kmanincraftghhj22/releases/download/render-238/thats-me-the-elbow.mp4) · [MP4 in this folder](thats-me-the-elbow.mp4) · [Source code](source/) · [49-shot sheet](shots.jpg)**

On November 18, 2026 the owner watched this cut: “the ears are creepy but its fine” and “i like it the humor is top tier.” The ears stay. This is the HUS film to study; the [original cook brief](../reference/OWNER-SCRIPT.md) stays in its own folder.

| Watch for | Where to look |
|---|---|
| Let the viewer find the joke. | In the first two seconds a moving birthday party becomes a print. The boy in green is **not** the narrator; look for the blue sleeve on the left. |
| Make each HUS an action you can point to. | Frames 91, 102 and 113: thumb on button, over the sofa, shoes on the X. Going faster gets him a great close-up of himself and **four ears**. Now he has to practise where to stand, not how fast to run. |
| Pay off the wish before the last joke. | Gran puts the five faces on the fridge. Then the couple hand him their camera. Hold on the unwanted third face in their print; nobody has to explain it. |

**Read the cut:** [script](source/narration.md), [timed storyboard](source/STORYBOARD.md), [captions](source/captions.srt), [encoded-film review](source/REVIEW.md). The 77.50-second film has 7 scenes, 49 setups and 242 recorded words. There are 24 HUS breaths in eight triples. The [mix lock](source/sound.lock.json) records 94 effects, −14.8 LUFS and −2.2 dB true peak. These are measurements of this cut, not audience results or quotas for the next one.
