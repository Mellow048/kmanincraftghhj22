# Idea: THE GUY WHO NEVER WENT TO PARTIES (THE BRIDGE)

**Status: IDEA, waiting for the owner's "go". Nothing is built.**

The owner brought the format from a reel and wrote his own version of it, *The Guy Who Never Went to Parties*
(theme: becoming a professional cook), which another agent is building. He asked this session for "an idea like
this", and to look the meme up first. This is the sister film: the same seven scenes, the same three catchphrases
("ALMOST, ALMOST!", "Training.", "HUS HUS HUS!"), a different skill.

## The meme, as looked up

| What | What it is | Source |
|---|---|---|
| "While they're out partying btw" | A TikTok and Reels trend: show a **useless** skill under that caption, the joke being that you perfected it while everybody else was out partying. Earliest known December 2025; big from August to October 2026; a Roblox clip under the caption took 8.5 million views in a week | https://knowyourmeme.com/memes/while-they-are-out-partying |
| Where it comes from | The 2016 line "While you were partying, I studied the blade": somebody deadly serious about something nobody asked for | same page |
| "Huss huss huss" | The sound of somebody **panting** while sprinting flat out. From the Huss Valley meme (July 2026, Daniel LaBelle's running clips). It is breath, not a battle cry | https://meme.com/memes/huss-valley |
| The Roblox version | Grind clips (basketball drills, e-bike wheelies) with "huss huss huss" over them and "while they're out partying btw" as the caption | https://www.tiktok.com/discover/paying-basketball-while-they-are-parting-huss-huss-eobloc |

What that means for the film:

1. **The skill has to look pointless** until the last scene. The dedication is far too big for it. That gap is the joke.
2. **He never finds it funny.** The narrator is sincere from start to end, like a sports documentary.
3. **HUS is him out of breath.** One HUS for each move, a puff of breath and a sweat drop with each one.
4. **"btw" is the smug bit.** The caption goes on screen once, over the training, with the party in the background.
5. The meme spells it **HUSS**. The owner's script spells it HUS. This idea keeps his spelling; the on-screen text can use either.

## Why bridging

Speed-bridging is the thing Minecraft kids really do grind alone for hours: walking backwards over nothing, crouched,
slapping a block under their own feet again and again. Outside one minigame it is useless. It has a rhythm (one HUS
a block), a failure everybody knows (you walk off the end), and a look that is funny on its own: a blocky man bobbing
up and down at a ridiculous speed.

# THE GUY WHO NEVER WENT TO PARTIES (THE BRIDGE)

**Target duration:** 1:40–1:50
**Theme:** Becoming the fastest bridge builder in town

## SCENE 1 — THE HOOK (0:00–0:03)

**Audio / Character:**
"HUS HUS HUS! HUS HUS HUS!"

**Visuals:**

- Extreme close-up of his face, bobbing up and down very fast. Sweat drops fly off it.
- Cut wide for one beat: he is walking backwards over a lake of lava, slapping blocks down under his own feet.
- One block for each HUS. Blocks and sparks everywhere.
- He slams the last block in with an incredibly fast final hit.

**SFX:** Rapid block placing.

*REWIND SOUND EFFECT.*

## SCENE 2 — EVERYBODY'S PARTYING (0:03–0:18)

Visual: A group of friends partying at a house. Music blasting, people dancing, a cake.

**Friend 1:** "BROOO! COME ON! YOU ONLY LIVE ONCE!"

**Friend 2:** "STOP BEING BORING! GET OVER HERE!"

Visual: Camera cuts to our main character alone at home, holding one block of cobblestone and looking at it.

**Narrator:**
"In this town, everybody loved partying. Every single night, my friends would invite me to come along. But I had a different dream."

## SCENE 3 — THE DREAM (0:18–0:30)

Visual: Main character watching a professional bridger on television, running backwards over nothing with a crowd cheering.

**Narrator:**
"I wanted to become the fastest bridge builder in town. I wanted to build a bridge so fast that people would never forget it."

Visual: He puts on a yellow hard hat, picks up a stack of cobblestone, and walks to the pond behind his house.

## SCENE 4 — THE FIRST FAILURE (0:30–0:43)

Visual: He places one block over the water and steps onto it, very proud.

He places the second block behind him, on the wrong side, and walks backwards off the end. Splash.

**Character:** "AHH! ALMOST, ALMOST!"

Visual: He floats in the pond and looks up at his bridge. It is one block long.

**Narrator:**
"Well... I still had a lot to learn."

## SCENE 5 — THE TRAINING ARC (0:43–1:12)

Visual montage:

**1. Placing blocks**

He stands at the edge of the pond with a stack of cobblestone.

He starts slowly: place, step, place, step.

Then progressively faster.

**Character:** "HUS HUS HUS! HUS HUS HUS!"

Visual: Blocks are flying in under his feet. The camera zooms in on his knees bobbing ridiculously fast.

**2. Bridging practice**

- He crosses the pond dry for the first time and throws both arms up. He falls in on the way back.
- He bridges over a ravine and misses one block. At the bottom is a hay bale with a dent in the shape of him.
- He bridges over lava, and his boots are smoking.
- The pond, from above: it is now covered in forty bridges.

On-screen caption, once: **WHILE THEY'RE OUT PARTYING BTW**. Behind him, the party house window is flashing.

**Narrator:**
"While my friends were out partying, I was practicing every single day. I fell in that pond hundreds of times, placed thousands of blocks, and spent countless nights on that bridge."

Visual: Calendar pages rapidly flipping.

**Narrator:**
"Months passed... and eventually, I was ready."

## SCENE 6 — HE GETS THE JOB (1:12–1:30)

Visual: Main character walking onto a huge building site in a hard hat and an orange vest.

**Narrator:**
"One day, I finally got hired by the biggest building crew in town."

Visual: The site is in chaos. A half-built bridge hangs over a lava canyon. A big sign says PARTY ISLAND, GRAND OPENING TONIGHT. One builder places a block a minute. Another drops his in the lava.

Our main character calmly tightens the strap of his hard hat.

Rapid montage:

- Placing blocks.
- Railings going up.
- Torches going on, one after another.
- A red carpet rolling out behind him.
- The last block.

Visual: The foreman looks at his work, absolutely amazed. His clipboard falls in the lava.

## SCENE 7 — THE REUNION (1:30–1:48)

Visual: His old friends walk over the brand new bridge towards the island, dressed for partying.

They notice someone at the far end, still placing blocks.

**Friend 1:** "WAIT... IS THAT HIM?!"

**Friend 2:** "BRO?! HOW DID YOU GET SO GOOD AT BUILDING BRIDGES?!"

Visual: Main character slowly turns around.

**Character:** "Training."

*Brief silence.*

Visual: Camera zooms into his face.

**Character:** "HUS HUS HUS! HUS HUS HUS!"

Visual: Rapid cuts of him bridging at ridiculous speeds. The last shot is wide: he bridges straight past Party Island, lights and music and all, and keeps going out over the sea.

He still has not gone to a party.

**END.**

## Two other skills, if bridging is not the one

- **The water bucket.** He jumps off his own roof with a bucket of water and tries to land in it. First failure: he lands next to it. Payoff: a friend falls off the balcony at the party and he gets there first.
- **Punching trees.** The first thing everybody does in Minecraft, trained like a martial art, bare hands. Payoff: the party house needs a new floor by tonight.

Four speakers: the narrator, two friends, and him (three short lines and the HUS). The friends and he need voices of
their own, which the owner picks from an audition.
