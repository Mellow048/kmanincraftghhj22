# Recreation: the shots

A Recreation film is built with `storyScene()`, one file for each of the seven scenes. `npm run check` holds every
shot to the base rules (a cue, a note that says why this picture, a moving camera, a `stage`). This page is what the
first film learnt on top of them.

| | Rule | Learnt from |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | **No picture over 3 seconds.** A cue that runs longer is cut into pictures with `cut:` | The meme is fast |
| 2 | **The chair shot stacks the heads.** The customer sits; the barber stands on a crate straight behind, higher. Side by side, one of them is always cut off by the tall frame | Stills of scenes 1 and 7 |
| 3 | **Hold things to one side of the face.** Scissors in front of a face that looks at the lens read as a tongue | The same |
| 4 | **Look at every piece of block text on a still.** Plates near the top of a wide shot get cropped; dark text on a pale board behind glass does not read. White or yellow on a dark plate does | The garage plate, the HELP WANTED board |
| 5 | **With the shaders on, an indoor lamp is 3 to 7.** At 16 and over the frame goes white. No bulb straight over a head, no big white thing near the lens, no emissive over 0.4 | Every interior of the first film, twice |
| 6 | **The camera for a queue stands at its head, on the street side, above it.** Along the wall, the first person hides the rest | Scene 6 |
| 7 | **A fast run changes one thing per picture.** A new head in the same chair, from the same camera | Scene 6 |
| 8 | **The hook and the reunion are the same pictures**, from the same cameras, so the loop is felt and not explained | Scenes 1 and 7 |

The chair and queue examples are from [the barber](../../src/videos/the-barber-who-never-went-to-parties/). For an approved HUS action run, compare the [Elbow storyboard](Hus%20hus%20reference/source/STORYBOARD.md) with the [decoded shot sheet](Hus%20hus%20reference/shots.jpg): the thumb presses, the kid clears the sofa, and his shoes land on the tape.
