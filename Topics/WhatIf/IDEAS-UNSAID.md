# What If — five premises, and the hooks for the one we are building

Read `Topics/MASTERPROMPT-01.md` first. Every premise below is checked against it: impossible fact in the first
second, a rule stated plainly, a cost, a turn, and a payoff you can see.

## The film we are building — "What If You Could Hear Every Unsaid Thing?"

**Premise.** From nine years old I hear every sentence a person decides not to say — in their own voice, only if
it was meant for me, gone in seconds. My dad has stood in my doorway every night for six years, deciding not to
say the same four words. I always assumed I knew what they were.

**Three hooks, and the one chosen.**

1. *"My dad has said the same thing to me every night for six years. He has never once opened his mouth."* — chosen.
   The impossible fact and the emotional fact arrive in the same breath, and it names a relationship rather than a
   power. Nothing needs explaining before the viewer wants to know why.
2. *"I can hear the sentence you just decided not to say."* — strong, but it makes the film a demo of a power.
   The viewer's next thought is "cool", not "who is this kid".
3. *"My teacher decided I would fail in the second week of school. She never said it out loud. I heard it."* —
   good scene, wrong opening: it spends the hook on a stranger.

**Retention plan.** The rule is stated at 0:05 so the audience can play along. Then the power is shown on four
people with four different flavours — a teacher (contempt), a friend (fear), a mother (money), a father (the
thing the film is about) — each one a new room and a new camera distance. The misuse beat (collecting secrets
"like coins") arrives at the halfway point, so the middle is never a repeat. The low point is the friend who moved
away: the power cannot answer, only hear. The turn re-reads the four words. The payoff is the first spoken
sentence in the film that the audience hears said *out loud*.

**Why the payoff lands.** The viewer has been hearing other people's private voices for seventy seconds. The last
voice they hear is the boy's, out loud, and it is the only one anybody else can hear. That is the whole film in
one gesture, and it costs nothing to shoot.

## Four more, for other agents

1. **What If Every Promise You Broke Fell Out Of The Sky?** It rains paper for four days, and each sheet is a
   promise somebody did not keep. A boy collects the ones with his father's handwriting and finds that every
   single one of them was made to himself.
2. **What If You Could See How Many Times Someone Practised Being Brave?** A number over every head, and the
   quietest kid in school has thousands. The bully has two.
3. **What If Your Shadow Was One Day Ahead?** Your shadow does tomorrow's movements today. The boy uses it to
   cheat, until he sees his shadow at a funeral.
4. **What If You Could Only Say A Thousand Words A Day?** Everyone has a counter. A father spends forty a day on
   himself and saves the rest for the one hour his son is awake.
