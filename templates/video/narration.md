# __TITLE__ — narration

Read `docs/NARRATION-RULES.md` before you write a word here. Then write the
spoken lines inside the `narration` block below — one line per beat, in order.

Rules, in one breath: one teller, one tense, plain words a 9-year-old knows,
3–10 words a line (12 is the ceiling), one idea per line. The image shows the
action; the line says what it means, what it costs, or what happens next.

`npm run check` lints this block: long lines and banned phrases (see
`docs/NARRATION-RULES.md`) show up as warnings.

```narration
The light was on.
He should have stayed in bed.
The door opened by itself.
Something was already in the hall.
```

Cut lines until only the ones that add a fact, a fear or a turn are left.
A 20-second short usually needs 4–7 lines. If you can mute the voice and the
film still makes sense, the voice is filler.
