**What changed from the pitch of idea 4:** it plays in an overworld town of endermen, not in the End, so nothing big
has to be built (the `Enderman` and the `Pumpkin` are in the pack). The players come for ender pearls, wearing
pumpkins, and the kid learns the trick by watching them. He is not given the pumpkin by his mum: he takes it.

**The recipe, and where each step is:** frame one is a famous mob and a kid doing the one thing a player would not
(a kid holds an enderman's hand and stares hard at the ground); line one is "I"; mum, dad and where he came from by
second 10 (endermen carry things home: "once, a baby"); a rule that looks cold and is not (nobody looks at him);
the thing the viewer usually is arrives as the danger (`4.night`, players); the small one stands up for his family
(`5.ran`); the game's own facts are the jokes and the plot.

**The Minecraft facts it turns on:** look an enderman in the eyes and it screams; wear a carved pumpkin and it
cannot tell; a pumpkin on your head blocks most of what you see; endermen pick blocks up and carry them; they
teleport; they will not go near water or rain; players hunt them for ender pearls.

**One sentence:** A kid raised by endermen, who has never seen his mum's face because looking makes her scream,
learns the pumpkin trick from the players who come to hunt his town, turns it on them, and looks up.
