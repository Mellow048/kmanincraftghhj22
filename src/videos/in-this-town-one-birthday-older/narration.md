```narration
# clip 01-hook
[1.rule]     In this town, everybody has two birthdays. Their own, and the Extra.
[1.week]     Mine is in March. My Extra is in April, and the whole street comes.
[1.count]    Twelve years old. Thirteen candles.
[1.chair]    And one more chair at the table than there are people in my house.

# clip 02-world
[2.why]      Nobody explains the Extra. Not my teacher. Not the man at the church.
[2.mum]      "Why the extra candle?" "Because it is the Extra." Then she cut the cake.
[2.slice]    The first slice goes on the plate at the empty chair. Nobody eats it.
[2.town]     Every house does it. I have watched it through every window on this street.
[2.cup]      There is a cupboard in the hall with a present in it for every year I have been alive.
[2.open]     I have never opened one. I asked once. My dad put the key in his pocket.
[2.worn]     The cushion on the empty chair is worn flat on one side. Somebody sits there a lot.
[2.plates]   Four plates for three people. Every night. Not only birthdays.

# clip 03-friend
[3.bea]      Bea lives on the corner. Her Extra is in June. Her present goes in a cupboard too.
[3.gift]     She has never looked at hers either. I thought she was scared of the dark.
[3.ask]      "Whose chair is it?" I asked her. "It is not a person," she said. That is worse.
[3.scared]   She was not scared of the chair. She was scared of me touching it.
[3.brother]  So I decided it was my brother. The one who came before. The one they wanted.
[3.jealous]  The dead one gets a cake and a present every year. I get a cupboard I am not allowed to open.

# clip 04-rule
[4.rule]     There is one rule at an Extra. You do not touch the chair.
[4.dare]     You do not blow the candle. You do not eat the slice. You do not move the plate.
[4.mum]      My mum watched me all night at my last Extra. Not the cake. Me.

# clip 05-break
[5.enough]   This year I had had enough of the dead one.
[5.blow]     I blew out the candle that was not mine.
[5.eat]      Then I ate the slice off the empty plate. It tasted like my own birthday cake.
[5.quiet]    The room went quiet the way a room goes quiet when a plate breaks.
[5.face]     My dad did not shout. He turned the chair to face the wall. Then the neighbours went home.

# clip 06-after
[6.morning]  In the morning, every house on the street had a chair facing the wall on its porch.
[6.walk]     Nobody said anything to me at school. Bea sat with somebody else.
[6.cupboard] I opened the cupboard with a spoon. Thirteen presents. All of them my size.
[6.tag]      Every tag said the same thing. "For the year you were given."
[6.mum]      My mum found me sitting under them. She sat down on the floor. "You are old enough now."

# clip 07-reveal
[7.deal]     In this town, when a baby comes too early, the town has one way to keep him.
[7.years]    Every family gives up one year. One birthday. One Extra.
[7.pool]     They pool them. They spend them on the child. That is the year he is not owed.
[7.me]       I was born at twenty-six weeks. The doctor gave me a night. Maybe.
[7.mine]     Every empty chair on this street is my chair. Every unopened present is a birthday I was not meant to have.
[7.why]      That is why nobody here is the age they should be. They are all one year short. I am the year.

# clip 08-list
[8.list]     The candle. The chair. The fourth plate. The cushion worn flat on one side.
[8.sat]      My mum sat in that chair every night for twelve years, so she could look at the door.
[8.gift]     The presents were never for a dead brother. They were every year I could have missed.

# clip 09-end
[9.back]     I did not open one of them. I put the key back in my dad's hand.
[9.cake]     At the next Extra I lit the candle, and my dad blew it out for me.
[9.age]      He is forty-four. He should be forty-five. He says he spent it well.
[9.like]     Like this video if somebody spent a year on you and never once mentioned it.
[9.loop]     In this town, everybody has two birthdays. Mine is the one they bought.
```
