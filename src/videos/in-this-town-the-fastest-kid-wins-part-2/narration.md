[1.town]     In this town, the fastest kid wins a trophy. And I was the slowest kid in town.

[1.second]   I came second at the rivers. And the kid who came first was wearing the same shoes.

# clip 02-tree

[2.baby]     Fifth was the baby. The one who used to beat me.
[2.friends]  Then two more came in. They were friends of first place. I looked at their feet.
[2.too]      They were wearing those shoes too!
[2.hide]     I got back behind the tree. I did not peek anymore.

# clip 03-paint

[3.why]      If they got found out, nobody would look at mine.

# clip 04-poop
