# Narration — What If Everybody Forgot You The Moment You Left The Room

## The four sentences

What if everybody forgot me the moment I left the room. I used it to get away with everything, and it was the best
week of my life. It worked, until I worked out that somebody was arranging the rooms. What it was really doing was
keeping me alive: my mum has been paying for it since the day I was born.

## The rule, in twelve words

**I leave a room, and everybody in it forgets I was there.**

The limit: it only works on rooms. Out of doors, under the sky, nobody forgets me — which is why the last act is
outside.

## The price

Nothing he does counts. He can be generous, funny, cruel, brilliant, and it is all deleted. He stops bothering to be
anybody, and the only person who ever remembers him is the one he cannot see.

## The five jokes, before 0:20

1. `2.food` — he eats, walks out without paying, and the canteen lady smiles at the next kid.
2. `2.tell` — the same teacher tells him off four times in a row and gets angrier every time.
3. `2.chair` — a whole class staring at an empty chair, terrified.
4. `3.five` — his mum sets five places at dinner for four people.
5. `3.shop` — he walks out of a shop with a bike and the man holds the door for him.

## The misread he says out loud

`4.best` — *"I thought it was the best thing that ever happened to me."*
