# In this town, the bell rang and nobody was allowed to look up

The hook is a law and a kid already breaking it. The bell rings once a day. The whole town puts its face in the dirt. He does not. He sees the thing the law was written for, and he cannot unsee it, and the bell is about to ring again.

## 1. The law

- 1.bell: In this town, the bell rang once a day, and nobody was allowed to look up.
- 1.dirt: Every face went into the dirt. Shopkeepers. Kids. The iron golem.
- 1.me: I was nine. I looked up.
- 1.saw: There was a hole in the sky, and something was climbing out of it.

## 2. What I saw

- 2.big: It was bigger than the bell tower. It had too many legs.
- 2.slow: It moved like it had all day. It did not see me.
- 2.down: Then the bell stopped, and I put my face in the dirt like everybody else.
- 2.fine: Nobody asked what I saw. You did not talk about the bell.

## 3. The next day

- 3.again: The next day I looked up again. It was closer.
- 3.roof: One leg was on the baker's roof. The baker had his face in his bread.
- 3.tell: I grabbed my friend. "Look up. Please look up."
- 3.wont: He would not. He put his hands over his eyes and sang the town song.

## 4. The rule, again

- 4.mum: I told my mum. She put a hand on my head and pushed it down.
- 4.dad: My dad said, "The bell keeps us. Do not make it ring twice."
- 4.why: "Keeps us from what?" He smiled. He did not answer.
- 4.night: That night I could not sleep. The thing was on the well. I heard it.

## 5. I stayed up

- 5.wait: So the next day I did not put my face down at all.
- 5.count: I counted the legs. Eight. Then nine. Then I stopped counting.
- 5.eye: It had one eye, and the eye was on the bell.
- 5.want: It wanted the bell. The bell was the only thing it could not stand.

## 6. The hand

- 6.reach: It reached for the rope. I was the only one standing.
- 6.run: I ran. Under its leg. Past the baker. Up the tower steps.
- 6.pull: I got to the rope first, and I pulled it.
- 6.twice: The bell rang twice. The whole town looked up.

## 7. They saw it

- 7.scream: My mum screamed. My friend stopped singing.
- 7.run: Everybody ran. Not away. Toward the tower.
- 7.rope: Hands on the rope. Ten of them. Then twenty.
- 7.ring: The bell did not stop. The thing hated it. It hated all of us now.

## 8. It left

- 8.back: It put one leg back into the hole. Then another.
- 8.slow: It went slow. It looked at me the whole way.
- 8.gone: The hole closed. The sky was just sky.
- 8.quiet: The bell stopped. Nobody spoke.

## 9. The new rule

- 9.mum: My mum looked at the sky for a long time. Then she looked at me.
- 9.new: "From tomorrow," she said, "we look up."
- 9.bell: The bell rang once, the way it always had.
- 9.up: Every face came up. The baker. The kids. The iron golem.
- 9.end: Like and subscribe for more.
