# Narration — My Dad Tried To Beat The Dragon Before I Woke Up

**Status: DRAFT. Not approved, not recorded.** Nothing is built until the owner approves the script.

**The premise, one sentence:** A dad who has never played Minecraft promises to beat the Ender Dragon before his
son wakes up, and digs four hundred blocks straight down to do it — because he is too frightened to look an
enderman in the eye, and ender pearls are the only way to the End.

**The spine** (nine beats, taken from the reference *His Dad Kept Punching The Same Tree For An HOUR..*):

| # | beat | s | the job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | hook | 0–7 | impossible premise + the person it matters to, then the mechanic |
| 2 | catch-22 | 7–18 | he needs a pearl, a pearl needs a look, he will not look |
| 3 | why | 18–28 | the guilt: "too slow" |
| 4 | grind | 28–40 | four hundred blocks, a torch every ten |
| 5 | heart | 40–50 | he used to be the one who said tomorrow |
| 6 | low | 50–58 | lava; the sign; silence |
| 7 | rescue | 58–66 | **role reversal**: the son tells him to look |
| 8 | payoff | 66–80 | he looks, it drops, they throw twelve eyes together |
| 9 | CTA | 80–82 | after the payoff, never during it |

## Three hooks, and the one chosen

**A. The promise, cold open.** *Visual:* a man in mismatched armour, lit by a monitor, punching dirt at the
bottom of a torch-lit shaft. *Narration:* "My dad has never played Minecraft. Last night he promised he'd beat
the dragon before I woke up." — Curiosity is double: an adult who cannot play, and a task he cannot finish. It
states the stake (a promise to his son) inside three seconds, and the shaft already shows *how* he is trying,
so the viewer trusts the film knows its own rules.

**B. The hole.** *Visual:* the camera drops down a four hundred block shaft, torch after torch. *Narration:*
"This is four hundred blocks deep. My dad dug all of it in one night." — Stronger image, weaker person. It
withholds the promise for a beat, and the reference's hook works because the promise arrives immediately.

**C. The enderman.** *Visual:* an enderman in a dark field; the dad's view snaps away from it. *Narration:*
"My dad is scared of one thing in Minecraft, and it's the only thing he needs." — Intriguing, but it opens on
game knowledge instead of a person, and a viewer who does not know what an enderman is has nothing to hold.

**Chosen: A.** It is the only one that has a person, a promise and a mechanic in the first three seconds, which
is the shape the reference opens on.

## The lines

Past tense, first person, spoken by the son. 27 stops. About 250 words, about 75 seconds.

```narration
# clip 01-hook
[1.never]     My dad has never played Minecraft. Not once.
[1.promise]   Last night he promised he'd beat the dragon before I woke up.
[1.down]      He thinks the way to the End is straight down.

# clip 02-catch
[2.needs]     To find the End you need twelve eyes of ender.
[2.pearls]    An eye of ender is made from an ender pearl.
[2.look]      And a pearl only drops if you look one in the eye.
[2.wont]      My dad will not look at one. Not for anything.

# clip 03-why
[3.slow]      Two years ago I told him he was too slow for this game.
[3.heard]     He heard it. He just never said he'd heard it.
[3.midnight]  So he started at midnight, with a wooden sword.

# clip 04-grind
[4.dug]       And he dug. Straight down, four hundred blocks.
[4.torch]     A torch every ten, so he wouldn't be scared.
[4.count]     He wrote the count on the wall like a calendar.

# clip 05-heart
[5.bed]       I used to ask him to play with me every night.
[5.tired]     He'd say tomorrow. He was always tired.
[5.now]       Now he's the one waiting on the other side.

# clip 06-low
[6.lava]      At four his torch found lava, and the shaft lit orange.
[6.quiet]     Six hours of digging, and then nothing at all.
[6.sign]      He left a sign at the top and went quiet.

# clip 07-rescue
[7.read]      I woke up and read it. Sorry. I tried.
[7.said]      Dad, I said. You don't dig to the End.
[7.look]      You look at it. Just look at it.

# clip 08-payoff
[8.turned]    He turned round. The enderman was right there.
[8.pearl]     And when he looked, it dropped a pearl.
[8.portal]    We threw twelve eyes at breakfast. Together.
[8.sword]     He hit the dragon last, with that wooden sword.
[8.sub]       Subscribe, if your dad would dig four hundred blocks for you.
```
