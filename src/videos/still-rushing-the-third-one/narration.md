# Still Rushing the Third One

Original popcorn training comedy. The owner approved the popcorn pitch and asked for the MP4. The basketball transcript is a rhythm reference, not dialogue to reuse. The teller is the only speaker; Bea answers with her cup, her pencil and her seat. One **HUS = one visible toss in practice**, never background chatter. The actual contest is played without the chant.

Selected hook: practice toss / catch / forehead ricochet (the skill and its flaw in the first shot). Alternatives: open on the gold-seat prize (no person yet) or the notebook (would explain the joke before we see it). Transfer from Monster Village 0:54–1:04: a change of viewpoint explains a gesture. Here the side view reveals the hand has started its next reach before the third throw is finished. The same notebook that diagnosed the problem becomes a seat wedge. No party invites, basketball win, goldfish or barber gag borrowed.
