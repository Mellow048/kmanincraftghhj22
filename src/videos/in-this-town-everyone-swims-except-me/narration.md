# Narration — In This Town, Everyone Swims Except Me

**Status: DRAFT from the owner's outline. Not recorded yet.** Built to the **middle cut** (`cut: "middle"`):
118–150 seconds, 500–640 words, one to two and a half minutes.

Topic: `Topics/InThisTown`. The outline is the owner's, word for word, in `Topics/InThisTown/outlines/`.
His plot, his order, his lines. Everything added is listed below.

**The premise, in four sentences.** In this town, we hold a swimming race every summer, and the winner gets a big
trophy. I can't swim — I can shower, but the moment the water passes my toes I panic like a salted worm. I trained
for four days, almost drowned twice, and learned just enough to stay on top of the water. Then a stranger in the
woods (a villager in a black suit) gave me a ring with three buttons on it, and the water stopped being a problem — until the race, when I saw
another ring on the hand in front of me.

**One sentence:** A kid who is scared of water gets a magic ring three days before the town's swimming race, and
finds out on the last length that somebody else has one too.

**Additions to the owner's outline** (everything else is his, beat by beat):

1. The trophy sits in the town hall window all year (`1.window`), so the want is a thing on screen from the start.
2. The pool smells like bleach and other kids (`2.smell`), and the little kids laugh (`2.laugh`).
3. The video he watches is called **"learn fast"** (`3.title`) — his phrase, made into a title card on the phone.
4. **Medium is too fast.** He tries the middle button, the pool "goes short", and it scares him (`6.scared`). That
   is why he is still on low in the race and cannot catch the leader (`8.dare`) — the owner's line, given a reason.
5. The friend jumps in with his shoes on (`4.shoes`); the ring is cold and hums (`5.cold`), and the friend is not at
   the pool the day it works (`6.alone`), so nobody sees it but him.
6. Number sixty-six (`7.number`), the trophy on a table by the water (`7.table`), and the three-pool announcement
   (`7.three`–`7.most`) are his; the lines around them are joins.
7. No explanation of the stranger, and no answer to "what is this for?" (`5.silent`). That is part 2.

**One teller, first person, past tense.** Fast and plain, about 255 words a minute. Each `[cue]` is one shot; cut on
the noun. The last line is his: "Like and subscribe for part two."

What is planted, and where it pays off:

| Planted | Pays off |
|---|---|
| The trophy in the town hall window (`1.window`) | On a table by the water, and he finishes second (`7.table`, `8.secondplace`) |
| The friend who pulls him out (`2.friend`, `4.pull`) | Not there the day the ring works, so the trick has no witness (`6.alone`) |
| "Learn fast" (`3.title`) | The ring does the learning; he never learns to float (`6.fast`) |
| Medium is terrifying (`6.scared`) | He stays on low in the race, and cannot catch the leader (`8.dare`) |
| The stranger says nothing, and the ring hums (`5.silent`, `5.cold`) | The leader's ring glints on the same hand (`8.glint`) — part 2 |
| "Most wins takes the trophy" (`7.most`) | Second place: the trophy is right there and he doesn't get it (`8.secondplace`) |
