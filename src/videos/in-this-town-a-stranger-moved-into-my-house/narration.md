**Unlike film 1 (the dog), and unlike the creeper and enderman films:** a grown villager, flat and sure of himself; an
ordinary village by day; the player is in frame one and is the joke; it is a list of complaints, a roast of the
viewer, and the turn is that the complainer had it backwards. It is funny all the way down, and the last line is a
punchline.

**The Minecraft facts it turns on:** players sleep in villagers' beds and take them, loot chests, trample wheat, take
the bell, dig straight down for diamonds, break and place a lectern until the librarian sells Mending, light
everything so monsters cannot appear, wall villages in, move villagers by boat, and cure zombie villagers with a
splash of weakness and a golden apple. A cured villager sells to him cheaply for ever. Killing a patrol captain gives
a player a bad omen: grey swirls round him, and a raid when he walks into a village.

**One sentence:** A villager lists everything a rude stranger has done to his town, gets every reason wrong, and
after the raid works out that it was never his town at all.
