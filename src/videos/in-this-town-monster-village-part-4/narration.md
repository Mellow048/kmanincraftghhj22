# clip 01-night
[1.town]     In this town, I was the only one who was not a monster. And I was in a cage.
[1.night]    It was night. Half of the castle was asleep.
[1.shift]    The other half was on the night shift.
[1.asleep]   I was asleep too. So I did not see any of this. They told me later.
[2.guard]    One guard woke up in his room. He could not sleep.
[2.sheep]    He looked at the ceiling. He counted sheep. It did not work.
[2.air]      So he went outside, for some fresh air.
[2.stars]    It was a nice night. There were stars. He took a big breath.

# clip 02-bush
[3.bush]     Then a bush moved.
[3.who]      "Who is there?" he said. The bush did not say.
[3.sword]    He pulled out his sword. He walked up to the bush.
[3.slow]     Slowly. Very slowly.
[3.bam]      BAM! It was my dad. With his jaw wide open.
[3.scream]   The guard screamed. It was a very long scream.
[3.sorry]    My dad was meant to be quiet. He put his jaw back. "Sorry," he said.
[3.lights]   The whole castle heard it. A light went on in every window.

# clip 03-tower
[4.tower]    Up on the tower, one player was asleep on the job.
[4.glass]    He woke up. He picked up his spyglass. He looked at the forest.
[4.trees]    Trees. Trees. Trees. Then the trees moved.
[4.pill]     Pillagers. Hundreds of them.
[4.gers]     And vindicators. And evokers. And witches. And some I do not know the name of.
[4.vex]      Then something small and blue filled the whole spyglass. A vex.
[4.face]     It flew right at his face.

# clip 04-wall
[5.woke]     That is when I woke up.
[5.fell]     Behind me, somebody fell off a tower. I did not see that either.

[5.steps]    It walked through the smoke. Every step shook my cage.
[5.angry]    The pillagers were not scared. They were angry. They opened a gate of their own.

[6.where]    "Where did you get that key?" I said.
[6.ask]      "Do not ask," he said.
