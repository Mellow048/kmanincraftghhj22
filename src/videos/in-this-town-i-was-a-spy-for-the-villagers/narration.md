# Narration — In This Town, I Was a Spy for the Villagers

**Status: APPROVED by the owner on 2026-11-15.** The exact user-authored hook and opening are preserved. The owner approved the required format prefix and the story-specific Subscribe button before production.

Topic: `Topics/InThisTown`. Short cut, `cut: "short"`, target below 118 seconds.

**The premise, in four sentences.** In this town, the bell is the village's warning. A villager disguises themself as a pillager to discover the raid plan. The pillagers will smash the bell tower at sunrise, so the villagers turn the ram's poor handling against them. The bell survives, but the spy still cannot walk like a pillager.

**One sentence:** A masked villager infiltrates a pillager camp, discovers a dawn plan to destroy the village bell, and uses the ram's clumsy turn to save it.

What is planted, and where it pays off:

| Planted | Pays off |
|---|---|
| The village bell is the first thing shown and named | The bell rings after the ram misses it |
| The spy wears a pillager mask and copies their march | The spy still marches stiffly into the hay at the end |
| The training ram nearly tips at a tight turn | The villagers build a zigzag that sends it into hay |
| Sunrise is marked beside the wooden target | The pillagers attack at sunrise |
