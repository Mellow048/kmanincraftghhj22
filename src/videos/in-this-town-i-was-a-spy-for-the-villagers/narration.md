8. **The cliffhanger** (`13.rain` to `13.quiet`): it rains, the ash runs off, and his arms cross by themselves.

**One sentence:** A villager who never volunteered sneaks into the pillagers' outpost in their clothes, is handed the
banner that leads the raid on his own town, and walks them in a circle until they burn down their own home.

# clip 01-hook
[1.town]     In this town, I was a spy.
[1.goal]     My goal was to see what the pillagers were planning.

# clip 02-why wait 0.35
[2.raid]     Every month, the pillagers came out of the woods and raided our town.
[2.took]     They took our bread. They took our emeralds. Once, they took our bell.
[2.when]     And we never knew when they were coming.
[2.need]     So the town needed a spy, to find out.
[3.asked]    They asked who wanted to go. Everyone took one step back.
[3.me]       I was looking at a butterfly.

# clip 03-inside wait 0.4
[4.outpost]  The pillagers lived in an outpost, deep in the woods.
[4.guard]    The guard at the gate was asleep standing up.
[4.start]    It all started when I sneaked inside
[4.clothes]  and grabbed some clothes to disguise me as a pillager.
[5.grey]     But pillagers are grey. I am not.
[5.ash]      So I rolled in the ash of their campfire.
[5.arms]     And pillagers carry crossbows. I had never uncrossed my arms in my life.
[5.hour]     It took me an hour.

# clip 04-line wait 0.4
[6.line]     In the morning, I stood in their line.
[6.stare]    One of them stared at my face for a long time.
[6.new]      Then he grunted at me. I grunted back. We were friends now.
[6.smart]    Pillagers are strong. They are not smart.
[6.wall]     One of them walked into a wall all morning. Nobody helped him.
[7.bow]      Then they gave me a crossbow.
[7.hat]      My first arrow knocked the banner right off the captain.
[7.point]    Everyone turned around. I pointed at the guy next to me.
[7.pond]     They threw him in the pond.

# clip 05-plan wait 0.45
[8.night]    That night, I went looking for the plan.
[8.table]    It was on a table, at the top of the outpost.
[8.map]      A map of my town, with a red X on our bell.
[8.dawn]     They were coming tomorrow, before sunrise. And they would take the bell first,
[8.warn]     so nobody could warn anyone.
[8.ravager]  And this time, they were bringing a ravager.
[8.window]   I had to get home. I climbed out of the window.

# clip 06-banner wait 0.45
[9.captain]  The captain was standing right under it.
[9.done]     I thought I was finished.
[9.banner]   He handed me the banner.
[9.rule]     The banner goes in front, and everyone follows the banner. It is the only rule pillagers have.
[9.grunt]    He said I had the best grunt in the outpost.
[9.lead]     So now I was leading the raid. On my own town.

# clip 07-march wait 0.5
[10.march]   We marched out in the dark. Forty pillagers, one ravager, and me in front.
[10.lights]  I could see the lights of my town. My mum had left one on for me.
[10.idea]    Then I had an idea. It was not a good one.
[11.left]    I turned left.
[11.follow]  They all turned left. Nobody asked why.
[11.again]   So I turned left again. And again. And again.
[11.night]   We walked all night.

# clip 08-raid wait 0.5
[12.sun]     At sunrise, there was a tower in front of us, in the fog.
[12.charge]  The captain yelled, and they charged.
[12.smash]   They smashed the gate. They burned the tower. The ravager ate the kitchen.
[12.cheer]   They cheered. Then they stopped cheering.
[12.own]     It was their own outpost.

# clip 09-rain wait 0.6
[13.look]    The captain looked at the tower. Then at the banner. Then at me.
[13.rain]    And that was when it started to rain.

[13.quiet]   Nobody grunted.
