# Hook and concept record

**Owner-approved idea, 2026-11-18; he explicitly authorized writing and producing the script without a second script review.**

## Premise
A kid overtrains carrying all the groceries in one trip, uses the absurd skill to help a neighbor, and discovers the one potato he left behind.

## Three openings

1. **Selected:** Begin mid-carry, with every bag and the eggs making it from trunk to house; potato plainly sits at the trunk edge, hidden from the kid by the load. Three breath-marks accompany the carry. Pixel sign: **ONE TRIP**. “One trip. Every bag. Even the eggs. How did I get it all inside without dropping a thing?” Curiosity: how the load stays intact, and why there is still one item in the trunk.
2. Begin with the full grocery load at the kitchen door, then the egg carton opens. “The door opened. The eggs did too.” Immediate embarrassment, but the failure rather than the skill becomes the opening picture.
3. Begin on the lone potato under the trunk blanket. “I almost made it in one trip.” The joke is instantly legible but spoils the final image.

## Spine
Hook: impossible grocery carry. Setup: sister makes four sensible trips. Objective: train for one. Complications: bags swing, produce rolls, egg carton opens. Escalation: re-pack and practice the door. Twist/useful payoff: the neighbor needs the skill at the buzzer. Final button: one potato makes him take a second trip.

## Ear constraint
No ear props, oversized or stacked ears, family-photo gag, or ear joke. The film uses only the engine's ordinary block-avatar heads.
