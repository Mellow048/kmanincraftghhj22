# Tariffs, Explained Using Minecraft

```narration
# clip 01-hook
[1.hook]       Tariffs, explained using Minecraft.
[1.jump]       One second this sword costs five emeralds.
[1.fifteen]    The next second, it jumps to fifteen.
[1.two]        Picture two Minecraft islands across one bridge.
[1.special]    Red Island bakes bread. Blue Island crafts swords.

# clip 02-cheap
[2.felint]     Meet Felint from Red Island.
[2.sam]        Local smith Sam charges twelve emeralds per sword.
[2.cost]       Sam pays high iron costs at home.
[2.cross]      Then Felint crosses over to the other side, Blue Island.
[2.five]       Blue smith Alex sells the same sword for five!
[2.deal]       Felint buys it and saves seven emeralds.
[2.rush]       Soon the whole town sprints across to shop.
[2.empty]      Back home, Sam stares at empty chests.
[2.close]      With zero buyers, Sam might shut his shop.

# clip 03-tariff
[3.beg]        So Sam runs straight to Mayor Sterling.
[3.idea]       Sterling smiles and slams down a red gate.
[3.rule]       He stamps one rule on every foreign sword.
[3.tax]        Ten extra emeralds just to cross this bridge!
[3.name]       That border tax on imports is a tariff.
[3.math]       Blue swords cost five, plus ten at customs.
[3.total]      Total price, fifteen emeralds at the counter!
[3.switch]     Now Sam's twelve emerald sword looks cheap.
[3.cheer]      Sam cheers as shoppers walk back inside.

# clip 04-tradewar

[4.notblue]    Not Blue Island. Alex still keeps five.
[4.felintpays] Felint pays more, even buying from Sam!

[4.after]      Now twelve is the cheapest sword left.
[4.mad]        Worse, Blue Mayor Cobalt spots the red gate.
[4.revenge]    He slams a revenge tax on Red bread!

[4.war]        Now both islands lock down in a trade war.
[4.stacks]     Prices spike while chests sit half empty.
```
