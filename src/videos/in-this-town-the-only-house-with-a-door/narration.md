# Narration — In This Town, The Only House With a Door

**Status: DRAFT 1 for the owner. Not approved, not recorded.** 46 shots, 603 words, ~145 seconds at 250 words a
minute. Built to the `WHY-IT-WORKED.md` skeleton, not the shadow film's.

One-shot. The premise: every house in this town has no doors — people walk through walls — and his is the only house
with one, locked from the inside. What he thinks is a punishment turns out to be the whole town standing between him
and the thing that knocks.

What is planted, and where it pays off:

| Planted | Pays off |
|---|---|
| The key he wears and is not allowed to touch (2.key) | It is warm because his parents hold it in turns all night (7.warm) |
| Light in the crack that is not the kitchen light (2.crack) | There is a street behind the door, and it is full of houses with doors (5.street) |
| Nobody else can see the door (2.why, 3.see) | Only the people who love you can see the door you need (7.list) |
| Four hours standing with their backs to it (4.four) | They are standing between him and the knocking (6.knock) |
| "It knocks in my voice" (6.voice) | The thing outside is what he would be with nobody in front of it (7.real) |
| Bea counting the door every morning (3.count) | She is the one who sees it too, which is why she never argued (7.list) |
| "No doors, no leaving" (2.rule) | The town has no doors so that this one door is the only way in (6.knew) |

```narration
# clip 01-hook
[1.town]     In this town, every house has a door. Nobody uses one.
[1.wall]     They walk through walls. My teacher walks through a wall with her tea. Nothing spills.
[1.ours]     Except my house. My house has a door.
[1.locked]   And it is locked. From the inside.

# clip 02-world
[2.why]      A door is a thing you cannot unsee. Nobody in town can see ours.
[2.mum]      My mum brings the plates in through the wall. My dad leaves the bathroom through the wall.
[2.six]      But at six every evening they both stop, and they look at it.
[2.key]      They gave me a key. I am not allowed to touch it. I wear it round my neck.
[2.crack]    There is a crack in the frame. The light in that crack is not kitchen light.
[2.rule]     The rule here is one line long. No doors. No leaving.
[2.kid]      I asked a boy about it. He said, "What is a door?" and walked into a wall.
[2.good]     Everyone laughed. I laughed too. I am very good at that.

# clip 03-friend
[3.bea]      Bea lives in the house next to mine. There is no wall between us. She walks in whenever.
[3.lunch]    She eats my lunch by walking through my bedroom wall. I let her. It is easier.
[3.see]      Bea cannot see the door either.
[3.count]    So every morning she counts it for me. "One door. Still there. Still locked."
[3.answer]   "Why do they lock it?" I asked. "So you stay," she said. That is not an answer.
[3.stare]    Then she did the thing I hate. She looked at the crack.

# clip 04-wrong
[4.ten]      Every night at ten, my parents stand in the hallway.
[4.back]     Backs to the door. Faces to the wall.
[4.four]     They stand there for four hours. They do not talk. They do not sit.
[4.wrong]    I thought I knew why. I was the weak one. The door was weak too.
[4.told]     I told Bea I was being guarded. She did not argue. That should have worried me.

# clip 05-break
[5.enough]   So one night I had had enough.
[5.key]      I took the key off the hook. It was warm.
[5.turn]     I turned it. Nobody woke up. That was the first strange thing.
[5.open]     I opened the door four inches.
[5.light]    Light came in like water through a hole in a boat.
[5.street]   There is a street outside. Houses. Our houses. And every house has a door.
[5.crowd]    And on that street, facing us, standing dead still, was the whole town.

# clip 06-them
[6.faces]    Every neighbour. My teacher. The boy who does not know what a door is.
[6.stand]    All of them looking at my door, at ten at night, in the cold.
[6.mum]      Then they turned. My mum put her hand flat on the frame and said, "We know."
[6.knew]     "We know what is behind it. We have no doors so that yours is the only one."
[6.knock]    "So we stand in front of it, every night, until it stops knocking."
[6.voice]    It knocks in my voice. Three times. It has always knocked in my voice.

# clip 07-list
[7.real]     The key. The warm key. Four hours on a cold floor.
[7.list]     The crack I was told to ignore. The light I was told was a lamp.
[7.bea]      Bea counting it every morning, so that I would know it was still shut.
[7.me]       Not one of them was guarding the house from me.
[7.keep]     They were keeping the whole town's only door shut. For me.

# clip 08-end
[8.close]    I closed it. I locked it. I put the key back on the hook.
[8.join]     Then I went and stood in the hallway with them, my back to the door.
[8.morning] In the morning Bea walked through my wall and ate my lunch, and I let her.
[8.like]     Like this video if somebody ever stood between you and the dark, and never said why.
[8.town]     In this town, every house has a door. Nobody uses one. Except when they use mine.
```
