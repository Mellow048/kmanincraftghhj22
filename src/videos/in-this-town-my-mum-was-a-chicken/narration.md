# Narration — In This Town, My Mum Was a Chicken

**Topic:** `Topics/InThisTown` (`cut: "short"`). Whole story in one film.

**One sentence:** A baby zombie ashamed of his tiny chicken mum discovers why she always watched him from high places when a cliff bridge breaks at sunrise and she glides the whole class to safety.

**Transfer Card (from Monster Village Part 1 + Owner Brief):**
- **Opening:** Frame 0 shows tall zombie mums holding zombie kids' hands at the school gate; by 0.5s the camera pushes onto the tiny baby zombie riding a white chicken with a pink bow as she lays an egg. No greeting, immediate Chicken Jockey visual.
- **Change of mind:** In beat 2 (`2.roof`, `2.under`) and beat 3 (`3.caught`), the teller thinks his mum likes high views and is weird for standing under him; in beat 5 (`5.float`) and beat 6 (`6.knew`, `6.under`, `6.wait`), we learn chickens float and take zero fall damage—she was waiting to catch him.
- **Choice & reward:** He overcomes his embarrassment, climbs onto her back at the broken cliff edge (`5.climb`), and she rescues all eleven kids before sunrise. Payoff: kids beg for rides (`6.next`), then the wobbling egg (`6.egg`) sets up `"Subscribe before it hatches."` (`6.sub`).
