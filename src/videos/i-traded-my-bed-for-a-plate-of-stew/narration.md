# Narration — I Traded My Bed For A Plate Of Stew

**A DEADPAN film (`Topics/Deadpan`). One short, one joke, six beats.**

Ten clips, 68 shots, ~370 words, ~1:45. Built to `Topics/Deadpan/SCRIPT.md`: the rule in the first two seconds, a
Chekhov's-gun stick and receipt, a five-stage ladder (bed → plate → table → floor → chair → arm → legs → mouth), the
pivot at 70%, a loophole that uses the antagonist's own rule, and an ironic button after the win.

**Premise.** In this village a trade is binding, for both sides, and anything that touched the traded item is part of
the deal. I traded my bed for a cold plate of stew and did not read the receipt.
