## 1. Title
**What If Your Shadow Did What You Were Thinking**

## 2. Premise
His shadow stops copying what he does and starts doing what he thinks — which is funny until it walks out of the room
without him, and it knows the way to a house he has never been to.

## 3. Three hooks, and the one chosen

**A. The shadow alone, waving at nothing (CHOSEN).** Frame one is a black figure on a board with its arm up, and nobody
in the shot. Curiosity: whose shadow is that, and what is it waving at? Chosen because the strange thing is on screen
before a word is said, which is what the reference does.

**B. The class assembly.** Every kid's shadow standing to attention while the kids slump. Rejected: it is a group shot,
and a group shot asks the viewer to find the joke instead of being given it.

**C. The shadow and the girl.** His shadow waving at a girl he is deliberately not waving at. Rejected as the opening
because it needs two characters and a relationship explained first; it became the second beat instead, where it lands
harder.
