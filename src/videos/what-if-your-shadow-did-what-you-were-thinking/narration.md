## The four sentences

What if my shadow did what I was thinking, instead of what I was doing. I used it to be honest without saying
anything, and it was the funniest week of my life. It worked, until my shadow started going somewhere I had never
been. What it was really doing was standing in a window for somebody who could not see me any more.

## The rule, in twelve words

**My shadow moves on its own, and it always tells the truth.** It cannot lie, and it cannot be stopped.

The limit: it can only go where I could go. It cannot open a door I have not been through, and it cannot speak.

## The price

Everybody can read him. So he stops thinking anything, to keep himself private — and then the shadow stops moving at
all. Being unreadable costs him the only friend he had, and then it costs him the shadow.
