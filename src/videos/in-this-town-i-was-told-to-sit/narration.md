**Not taken from Monster Village 1, or from the creeper and enderman films:** no kid, no mob mum and dad, no basket,
no friend in disguise, no players hunting at night. Who tells it: a dog. Where: one spot. What goes wrong: he waits,
and then two players want a bed. What the player is: the one he loves. How it ends: his owner comes back with
nothing. How it feels: funny, then a lump in the throat.

**The Minecraft facts it turns on:** a dog told to sit stays where he is; a dog who is not sitting follows his owner;
skeletons run from dogs; a parrot copies the sounds of monsters; torches keep monsters from appearing; twelve eyes of
ender open the way to the dragon; a player who dies comes back at his bed with nothing, and without the bed he comes
back somewhere else; players take beds.

**One sentence:** A dog who was told to sit four hundred days ago, and thinks he was forgotten, stands up for the
first time to save his owner's bed, and finds out what the bed, and "sit", were for.
