**The premise, in four sentences.** The old man's robot was taken from him years ago, so how did it land on the
teller's doorstep? Pip's glasses say there is only one such suit, and the old man is wearing a copy he spent forty
years building. He comes to town to take the real one back, and the teller and Nora beat him by bouncing a beam off
her shield. Beaten, he says the town never took it from him: the suit left him and picked the teller, and it will
leave the teller too.

**One sentence:** A kid finds out nobody chooses their robot, the robot chooses them, and his has already left one
owner.
