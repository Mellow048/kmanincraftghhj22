# Narration — In This Town, My Brother Got Bitten

**Status: RECORDED AND BUILT (2026-11-17).** The owner chose the idea from `Topics/InThisTown/outlines/ideas-2026-11-17.md`
with "yes go" and did not read the script first: it was built straight through, like the five films before it.

Topic: `Topics/InThisTown`. Written to `Topics/InThisTown/MASTERPROMPT.md` and the carry list of the Monster Village
study (`Topics/InThisTown/reference/monster-village/README.md`). `cut: "middle"`.

**One sentence:** A kid's big brother is bitten saving him, and the kid finds out that the barred room under the house
is not a cell: it is where half the town was cured.

**The premise, in four sentences.** In this town, somebody who is bitten goes out of the gate before sunrise. My
brother was bitten pulling me in through the door, and dad carried him out. He was in the cellar, and he still knocked
twice. The cellar was built for this, and dad knew how because it was once done for him.

## Three hooks, and the one chosen

| | Picture | Words | The question it leaves |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | The gate at dawn, a green villager walking out of it alone | "In this town, if you got bitten, you left before sunrise." | Who left? |
| B | A kid's ear against the floorboards; two knocks shake the dust | "In this town, something under my floor knocked twice." | What is under the floor? |
| **C (chosen)** | The kid, facing the lens, in daylight. His brother pops in beside him, then turns green; the town turns its back; bars drop in front of the brother; his fist knocks twice | "In this town, my brother got bitten. Everybody said he was gone. He was in our cellar. And he still knocked twice." | Is he still his brother, and can he be got back? |

C is chosen because the first four words name a person to care about and the first second shows both of them. A is an
empty establishing shot by the master prompt's rule. B hides the brother, who is the reason to stay.

## What is planted, and where it pays off

| Planted | Pays off |

## Minecraft facts the story stands on

A zombie villager is cured with a splash potion of weakness and then a golden apple; it shakes with red swirls for
three to five minutes; iron bars and beds nearby make it a little quicker; it burns in daylight; a cured villager sells
cheaper to whoever cured him. A potion of healing hurts the undead. Sources and what was not looked up:
`Topics/InThisTown/outlines/ideas-2026-11-17.md`.
