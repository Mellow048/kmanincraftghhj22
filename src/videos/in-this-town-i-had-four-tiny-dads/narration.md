# Narration — In This Town, I Had Four Tiny Dads

**Status: Approved & recorded.**

Topic: `Topics/InThisTown`. Written to `MASTERPROMPT-01.md`, `Topics/InThisTown/OWNER.md`, and the transfer card of
`Topics/InThisTown/reference/monster-village/NEW-SHORT.md`. `cut: "short"` (complete story in one film, no cliffhanger).

**The premise, in four sentences.** In this town, every kid had one big slime dad, except me—mine had split into four
tiny ones who barely reached my knees. He told me he just kept tripping down the stairs, and at school I made them stop
at the corner so nobody saw us. When I unbarred the old river bridge at sunset to prove I did not need guarding and woke
the iron golem on the other side, the dents by our porch finally made sense. Twice my dad had let that golem break him
in half to save me, and with zero splits left, all four of him still hopped right in front of my shoes.

**One sentence:** A boy raised by a slime dad who split into four harmless tiny slimes finds out why his dad is really
small when an iron golem wakes up across the barred swamp bridge.

## Minecraft facts the story stands on

A large slime splits into two medium slimes, and two medium slimes split into four tiny slimes; a tiny slime does not
split again and drops slimeballs when defeated. Large and medium slimes deal contact knockback/damage, while tiny slimes
deal zero damage. Iron golems ignore peaceful human players unless provoked, attack slimes of any size on sight, and do
not float or swim—they sink straight to the bottom of deep water. Nine slimeballs craft a Slime Block, which removes all
fall damage and bounces whoever lands on it.
