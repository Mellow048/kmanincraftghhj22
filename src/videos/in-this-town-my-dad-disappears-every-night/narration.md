# Narration — In This Town, My Dad Disappears Every Night

Topic: `Topics/InThisTown` (`cut: "short"`). Standalone story — no cliffhanger, no part two.

**Premise:** In this town, my dad disappears every night. He is a wandering trader with terrible trades and two goofy llamas, and every sunset he drinks an invisibility potion and vanishes. I thought he hid in shame — until I splashed a milk bucket on a floating torch and caught him fixing every broke neighbor's roof for free.
