# Freeze Bell — owner-directed look-up ending

## Decision

The owner liked the story but rejected the sudden “Subscribe before I get caught” ending. The proposed rescue-and-breakfast resolution was **not selected**. The owner instead asked for the protagonist to remember the golem looking down, look toward the sky, begin something like “He saw…”, then cut. They explicitly did not want a “subscribe for part two” promise. When asked which character, they repeated **“the golem who is looking down.”**

The film keeps its established first-person voice:

> Then I remembered the golem. He never looked up, even when the bell was quiet. So I looked up. And I saw...

**Cut to black. No CTA or sequel promise.** The image stays with his reaction; it does not decide or reveal what is above him.

## Implementation

- An actual approved pack `IronGolem`, rather than renaming the giant boy. It was not in v1, so plant it clearly in the town tour: **“Our golem kept looking down, even after the second ring.”** People behind it have restarted; its eyes stay down.
- Match that shot in a short memory at the end, then return to the same kid and jar on the shelf. No teleport, completed rescue or breakfast sequence is implied.
- Preserve the already-liked toy-box story and its bell explanation. Do not use the earlier proposal's cuts, altered lid closure or epilogue.
- Keep the bun physically in hand during the reach and upward look. Improve the final face/gaze framing, not the shared approved character designs.
- Add a frame-accurate black-cut option to the existing engine black transition. Its default is off; prove that the approved model-film samples render identically before and after.
- Keep the topic's normal CTA rule, with an owner-reasoned exact final-line exception for this film only. Test that the exception cannot waive another film's ending or allow an arbitrary replacement line.
