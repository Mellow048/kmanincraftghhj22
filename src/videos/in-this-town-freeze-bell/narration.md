# In This Town, We Had to Freeze Whenever the Bell Rang

**Owner-directed v2: the golem's downward gaze, an upward look, and an unfinished “And I saw…” ending. No subscribe line and no promised part two.** The owner liked the original story but rejected the sudden subscribe interruption. They did not select the proposed rescue-and-breakfast ending; they supplied the look-up cliffhanger instead and clarified that its clue is the downward-looking golem.

**Format:** the full InThisTown film, one kid in first person and past tense. The original story and toy-box revelation stay. The revised script has **724 words, 18 beats, 86 shots and 10 recording groups**. Seven recordings are reused unchanged. The selected narrator remains **`voice-00`**.

**Measured production:** **171.5667 seconds (2:51.6), 5,147 frames at 30 fps, 253.9 WPM**. All ten clips are aligned; `timing.json` has `estimated: false`. Shot lengths are **0.70–4.9667 seconds**, median **1.90 seconds**. The cue-aligned music/effects mix has been rebuilt for this script. Recording alignment and mix measurements are not a listening review. Current picture review and delivery status are in `PRODUCTION.md`.

**Premise:** A kid discovers why his miniature town freezes whenever a bell rings. After learning that their home is in a child's toy box, he recalls the golem who never looks up—even when the bell is quiet. He finally looks up himself. The film cuts before showing what he sees.

Only the words after each cue are spoken. Each cue is one shot. Clip headings are recording groups, not scene boundaries.

## Clues and payoffs

| Planted | Paid back |
|---|---|
| Unknown ringer, the scrape, and the rope through the wall | The toy-box lid and its physical bell linkage (`17.box`–`17.second`). |
| Heads down, moving shadows, and two strikes | The town passes as toys while the child has the lid open. The second strike marks closure, not safety for a person still outside. |

| The bun he always saved | The kid packed it for him and holds it out at the jar. The new ending does not falsely claim a completed rescue. |
| **The golem keeps looking down after everyone else restarts (`3.golem`)** | A brief memory of that exact pose (`18.down`) motivates the kid's own upward look. What he sees remains deliberately offscreen. |
| “What did we look like when none of us moved?” | “We had been pretending to be toys.” The later open question does not remove this completed reveal. |
