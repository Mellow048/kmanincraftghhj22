# Narration — Nobody Joined My Brother's Server For Three Years

**Status: DRAFT. Not approved, not recorded.** Nothing is built until the owner approves the script.

**The premise, one sentence:** Three years after his brother moved out, a kid keeps his brother's Minecraft
server alive — one block a night on a castle nobody else can see — and on the night it is due to shut down, a
second name appears in the player list.

**The spine** (nine beats, taken from the reference *His Dad Kept Punching The Same Tree For An HOUR..*):

| # | beat | s | the job |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | hook | 0–6 | impossible premise: a server with one player, for three years |
| 2 | what he left | 6–16 | the half-finished castle, the chest, the bed |
| 3 | the promise to self | 16–26 | I pay for it; one block a night |
| 4 | grind | 26–38 | three years of blocks; the roof |
| 5 | low | 38–48 | the server shuts down Friday; the sign |
| 6 | rescue | 48–58 | **role reversal**: the brother is the one who was watching |
| 7 | payoff | 58–72 | he places the last block |
| 8 | CTA | 72–74 | after the payoff |

## Three hooks, and the one chosen

**A. The player list.** *Visual:* a dark room, a monitor, a server list with one green name and the counter
"1095 days". *Narration:* "My brother moved out three years ago. His server still shows one player online every
night." — Opens on a number and a mystery the viewer can read in one second, then answers it in the third line
("It's me"), which is the same move the reference makes: state the impossible thing, then immediately show how.

**B. The castle.** *Visual:* a huge half-finished castle at sunrise, one block being placed. *Narration:*
"Every night for three years I've added one block to a castle nobody can see." — Beautiful image, but it withholds
the brother, and the story is about who it is for.

**C. The shut-down.** *Visual:* a countdown on a screen: SERVER CLOSES IN 3 DAYS. *Narration:* "On Friday the
server dies, and my brother still doesn't know I've been here." — Strong urgency, but it opens on plot, not on a
person, and the three years do not land until later.

**Chosen: A.** One player online for three years is the hook the whole film is about, and "It's me" is the turn.

## The lines

Past tense, first person, spoken by the younger brother. 26 stops. About 220 words, about 65 seconds.

```narration
# clip 01-hook
[1.left]      My brother moved out three years ago.
[1.online]    His server still shows one player online. Every night.
[1.me]        It's me.

# clip 02-what
[2.castle]    He left a castle half finished on a hill.
[2.chest]     A chest with his name. A bed with his name.
[2.stopped]   He stopped building the day he stopped talking to us.
[2.seed]      I know the seed by heart. I've never told anyone.

# clip 03-keep
[3.pay]       I pay for the server out of my lunch money.
[3.night]     Every night I log on and stand in the same spot.
[3.block]     And I add one block to his castle.

# clip 04-grind
[4.two]       One block a night. Three years of blocks.
[4.mobs]      I keep the torches lit and the creepers out.
[4.roof]      Last month I finished the roof he never started.
[4.never]     He never logged on. Not once in three years.

# clip 05-low
[5.birthday]  The server shuts down on his birthday. That's Friday.
[5.sign]      So I put a sign at the spawn point, just in case.
[5.words]     It's still here. I kept it.

# clip 06-rescue
[6.friday]    Friday came. One player online. Me.
[6.two]       Then the list moved. Two players.
[6.name]      His name. After three years, his name.
[6.wait]      I sat there for an hour without moving.

# clip 07-payoff
[7.walked]    He walked the whole castle without saying a word.
[7.block]     Then he put down one block. The last one.
[7.said]      He said: I watched the map. Every Sunday.
[7.light]     And the castle was finished. By both of us.
[7.sub]       Subscribe, if you'd keep the lights on for someone.
```
