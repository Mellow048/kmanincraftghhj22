[1.hook] Monopolies, explained using Minecraft.
[1.three] Three bakers sell bread in the square.
[1.two] Every loaf costs just two emeralds.

[1.best] If one raises prices, Miles walks next door.

# clip 02-buyout
[2.rich] Then Sterling shows up with a full chest.
[2.buy1] He buys the first bakery and shuts it.

# clip 03-shock
[3.walk] Miles walks up hungry for a loaf.
[3.flip] The price sign flips from two to fifteen.
[3.same] Same bread. Same square. Fifteen emeralds.
[3.what] So what happened?
[3.choice] Yesterday Miles had three choices. Today he has one.

# clip 04-power
[4.pay] If Miles wants bread, he pays Sterling's price.
[4.cheap] Worse, Sterling stops baking fresh bread altogether.
[4.stale] He sells dry crumbs instead of warm loaves.
[4.why] Why try hard when nobody can leave?

# clip 05-define
[5.name] That's a monopoly.
[5.rule] One seller owns the whole market alone.
[5.gone] Competition disappears completely.
[5.scale] Fifteen emeralds used to buy seven loaves.

[5.vault] Meanwhile, emeralds rain straight into Sterling's vault.
