# Narration — In This Town, Memories Were Money

**Status: Approved for narration and film.** The owner asked to hear the TTS and make the MP4. The narration below is the script presented to the owner, with punctuation normalized for alignment and shot cues added; no spoken words have been changed.

The short cut: first-person, 14 scenes. One cue is one moving picture; each clip is one recording.

**Story:** A boy sells his memories of his sister to feed his family, then chooses her life over getting those memories back. A paper boat helps them start again.

| Planted | Payoff |
|---|---|
| Nobody gets a memory back; the shop shakes | A press is crushing memories into emeralds |
| The sister's yellow scarf | It catches in a gear; freeing her costs the jar |
| Her paper boats float while his sink | He recognizes her boat under the door; they fold new ones at the river |
| Every night she leaves him a boat | He gives it back when she becomes a stranger |
| The shopkeeper's jars | The golem falls and breaks the other jars, freeing their memories |
