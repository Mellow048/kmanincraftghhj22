# In This Town, Nobody Turns On a Furnace

> **In This Town (Short Cut — Snow Golem Parents):**
> - **Frame-1 Daylight Hook:** Bright sunlit green square covered in snow trails behind two towering Snow Golem parents.
> - **100% Vanilla Mechanics:** Snow trails, pumpkin head disguise, burning/melting near heat/furnaces, 0-damage snowball knockback.
> - **Fresh Climax (Not a night raider clone):** Daytime bakery furnace fire -> Snow Golem Dad runs straight into the flames, melting block-by-block to extinguish the blaze and save the kids.

```narration
# clip 01-hook
[1.town]    In this town, nobody ever turns on a furnace.
[1.trail]   Every morning, my parents leave white snow trails across our front lawn.
[2.cold]    Inside our house, there are zero torches, zero stoves, and zero fireplaces.
[2.food]    While other kids eat warm baked potatoes, we eat frozen berries for dinner.

# clip 02-school
[3.school]  At school, kids laugh when my jacket is covered in frost.
[3.why]     Why can't we have one warm room like every normal house?
[4.dad]     My dad wears a carved pumpkin all day and barely says a word.
[4.mum]     Whenever I ask for a campfire, my mum quietly locks the flint away.

# clip 03-summer
[5.heat]    Then the hottest summer week hits our village, and every leaf turns dry.
[5.spark]   A player cranks the bakery furnace to full blast for a giant cake.
[6.fire]    One spark jumps onto the oak roof, and the whole bakery catches fire.
[6.trap]    Me and three other kids are trapped behind the burning wooden counter.

# clip 04-rescue
[7.run]     The villagers drop their buckets and back away from the blazing heat.
[7.dad]     Then my Snow Golem dad sprints straight through the burning doorway.
[7.melt]    His snow starts melting the second he steps inside, dropping block by block.
[7.throw]   He throws snowball after snowball at the flames until his arms nearly vanish.

# clip 05-payoff
[8.clear]   The last flame goes out, and all four of us scramble safely outside.
[8.puddle]  When I look back, only his pumpkin and one tiny snow block remain.
[9.save]    I grab snow off the ground and stack him right back up beside me.
[9.love]    He kept our house freezing just to stay strong enough for us.
[10.sub]    Subscribe if you didn't expect a snowman to beat fire.
```
