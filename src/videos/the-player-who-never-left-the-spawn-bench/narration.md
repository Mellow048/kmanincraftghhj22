# The Player Who Never Left The Spawn Bench

```narration
# clip 01-hook
[1.bench] There was a player who never left the spawn bench.
[1.four] Every day at four, Maya logged in and sat down.
[1.hi] She never mined diamonds, and never fought a mob.
[1.chat] All she ever typed in chat was hi.
[1.back] And whenever someone returned, she typed welcome back.

# clip 02-weird
[2.hut] She didn't even build a tiny dirt hut.
[2.walk] Players ran past her wearing full shiny diamond armor.
[2.wave] Maya just stayed on that oak bench and waved.
[2.bot] People started joking she was a lazy AFK bot.
[2.why] Nobody once stopped to ask why she never moved.

# clip 03-grief
[3.jake] Then one afternoon, a griefer named Jake got annoyed.
[3.tnt] He dropped red TNT right under Maya's wooden bench.
[3.boom] Boom! Wood splinters flew across the whole spawn square.
[3.type] Jake laughed and typed, go actually play the game.
[3.stand] Maya stood still in the crater for ten seconds.
[3.sorry] She slowly typed one word in chat: sorry.
[3.left] Then Maya disconnected, and didn't log back on.

# clip 04-reveal wait 0.5
[4.days] Three days passed, and the bench spot stayed empty.
[4.join] Then Maya's account joined, and her older sister typed.
[4.hands] Maya's hands shake too hard to hold a mouse.
[4.keys] She can only press two keyboard keys at once.
[4.desk] At school, kids stared whenever she dropped her pencil.
[4.only] That spawn bench was the one place nobody stared.
[4.loved] Saying hi to everyone was her favorite part of day.

# clip 05-guilt wait 0.5
[5.quiet] The entire server chat went dead silent for minutes.
[5.jake2] Jake stared at the crater he blew up.
[5.owner] Then the server owner typed: grab your oak planks.

# clip 06-rally
[6.forty] Forty players showed up at spawn that same night.
[6.build] They rebuilt her oak bench with warm glowing lanterns.
[6.plate] They wired a pressure plate right by her seat.
[6.auto] One tiny tap auto-typed welcome back in chat!
[6.town] Then they built a whole cozy village around her.

# clip 07-payoff wait 0.45
[7.back2] The next afternoon at four, Maya logged back in.
[7.line] Forty players were lined up, waving and cheering.
[7.flowers] Jake walked up and dropped bright red flowers down.
[7.glad] He typed, glad you're back, and sat beside her.
[7.home] Maya didn't type sorry—she just typed, I'm home.
[7.cta] Subscribe if you'd save Maya a seat, too!
```
