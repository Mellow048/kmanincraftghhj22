# Narration — My Dad Hasn't Left The Doorway In Six Years

**Status: scripted. One short, one story, no part two.**

Nine clips, ~38 shots, ~230 words, ~62 s. Built to `Topics/Stories/REFERENCE-STUDY.md`: the impossible fact in
the first second, the game rule explained before the joke is spent, a debt that is never stated out loud, a
visible time card, the low point held on a face, and a CTA that names the theme.

**Premise, three sentences.** My dad found out that a mob will not path through a one-block gap with a player in
it. So he stood in our doorway every night for six years, and I grew up behind him. The one thing in his
inventory is a wooden sword with one hit left — saved for the night I stop being scared.

**The rule.** A monster cannot walk through a player standing in a doorway. So the man never moves, and the boy
never sees him move.

| Planted | Pays off |
|---|---|
| He stands in the door and never turns around (1.door, 3.never) | Six years of a father facing outward, and a garden he never saw behind him (7.things) |
| A wooden sword with one hit left (4.sword) | He'd been saving that hit for his last step out of the door (8.used) |
| "The night you stop being scared" (5.you) | The boy is sixteen and has been brave for years; nobody told the father (5.nobody) |
| The zombie stops at the light and leaves (2.saw, 2.left) | The rule is real, so the father's fear was never the monsters (6.scared) |

```narration
# clip 01-hook
[1.door]     My dad hasn't left our doorway in six years.
[1.why]      Not because he's stuck.
[1.rule]     Because monsters can't walk through a player.
[1.explain]  A mob sees a body in the gap, and turns around.

# clip 02-night
[2.first]    The first night, a zombie came up our path.
[2.saw]      It saw him.
[2.left]     Then it walked away.
[2.stayed]   My dad stayed in the door.

# clip 03-behind
[3.grew]     I grew up behind him.
[3.floor]    I built a second floor. He heard every nail.
[3.never]    He never turned around once.
[3.busy]     I asked him to come and see. He said he was busy.

# clip 04-inventory
[4.check]    Last week, I checked his inventory.
[4.nothing]  No axe. No food. No bed.
[4.six]      Six years, and he carried one thing.
[4.sword]    A wooden sword with one hit left on it.

# clip 05-answer
[5.asked]    I asked him what he was saving it for.
[5.you]      He said, the night you stop being scared.
[5.sixteen]  I'm sixteen. That was a long time ago.
[5.nobody]   He didn't know. Nobody ever told him.

# clip 06-swap
[6.stand]    So I stood in the doorway beside him.
[6.hand]     I put my hand on his shoulder.
[6.swap]     And we swapped places.
[6.freeze]   Six years of standing still, and suddenly he could move.
[6.scared]   He was more scared of the yard than of the monsters.

# clip 07-garden
[7.said]     I said, Dad. It's just grass.
[7.step]     He took one step.
[7.another]  Then another.
[7.saw]      Then he turned around.
[7.things]   A garden. A pond. Two chairs.

# clip 08-morning
[8.sun]      He sat in the sun for the first time in six years.
[8.sword]    He put the sword on the table between us.
[8.onehit]   One hit left, after all that time.
[8.used]     He'd been saving it for his last step out.
[8.now]      He said, I didn't need it after all.

# clip 09-sub
[9.sub]      Subscribe if you'd take a shift.
```
