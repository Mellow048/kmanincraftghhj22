# The Player Who Only Built In Grey Wool

```narration
# clip 01-hook
[1.grey] This player only built with grey blocks.
[1.only] No wood, no brick, just grey wool.
[1.ugly] Everyone called his base the ugliest build.
[1.promise] When they learned why, they rebuilt it in color.

# clip 02-evidence
[2.rainbow] Everyone else built bright, colorful castles.
[2.box] Next to them sat Sam's grey wool box.
[2.sign] Players put a sign outside saying boring box.
[2.laugh] They laughed in chat at his grey walls.
[2.quiet] Sam never argued. He just kept building.

# clip 03-truth
[3.lazy] But Sam wasn't lazy or trolling.
[3.blind] He was born completely colorblind.
[3.shades] Red, green, and brown all looked grey.
[3.desk] At his desk, he had to guess blocks.
[3.school] Kids laughed when he drew a green sky.
[3.here] Here, grey meant he never got it wrong.

# clip 04-break
[4.prank] One night, a player snuck to Sam's base.
[4.swap] He swapped every wall with bright pink wool.
[4.chat] Fixed your ugly house, he laughed in chat.
[4.stare] Next morning, Sam stared at his doorway.
[4.cant] He typed, I can't tell what you did.
[4.quiet2] The whole chat went completely quiet.
[4.left] Then Sam logged off and didn't come back.

# clip 05-owner
[5.dm] So the owner messaged Sam right away.
[5.told] Sam told the owner everything.
[5.idea] And the owner knew what to do.
[5.texture] In Minecraft, every block has its own pattern.
[5.brick] Bricks have lines, logs have bark, glowstone shines.

# clip 06-rally
[6.join] That night, the whole server logged on.
[6.wall] They built a giant palette wall beside his yard.
[6.labels] Every row had a chest and color sign.
[6.rebuild] They rebuilt his home with brick, oak, and lanterns.

# clip 07-payoff
[7.return] When Sam logged back on, he saw the wall.
[7.chest] He opened each chest and read every sign.
[7.tears] For the first time, he saw every color.
[7.typed] Sam smiled and typed, now I see color too.
[7.gallery] The boring box sign became Sam's Gallery.
[7.cta] Subscribe if you'd help Sam build in color, too.
```
