# Narration — What If Every Lie Added a Netherite Block?

**Status: complete, standalone story draft for review.** This is one whole film, not a series. The clip headings below are voice-recording chunks only.

## The premise

Every deliberate lie added one Netherite block to my pack; only I could see the blocks, but I felt their real weight. I wanted my best friend to think I could build the brightest lantern for the school parade. After I broke its redstone switch, I kept lying until the weight threatened my life and left my friend holding the lantern alone. Telling him the truth cleared the blocks; admitting I could not fix it alone gave us a way to save the lantern together.

**The rule:** Every deliberate lie adds one Netherite block to the liar's invisible pack, and its weight is real. Telling the person you lied to the truth removes that lie's block.

**The limit:** The blocks never reveal which lie they came from or what truth to tell. If the weight keeps building, the liar can pass away.

**The price:** The narrator gets slower and weaker, just when his friend needs help.

**The plant and payoff:** His friend says, "If it breaks, we'll fix it together." The narrator says he can handle it alone. At the climax, admitting that he needs help removes the final weight.

Minecraft does not assign official real-world weights to blocks; Netherite's extreme weight is a magical rule of this story.

# clip 03-cover-up

# clip 04-the-storm

[12.truth]   I lied; the switch wasn't warming.
[12.fine]    I wasn't fine; I was scared.
[12.help]    I couldn't fix it alone. I needed you.
[13.release] The last three blocks disappeared; he pulled me up.
[13.repair]  Together, we repaired the switch and steadied the lantern.
[13.light]   It lit crookedly, but the whole street cheered.
