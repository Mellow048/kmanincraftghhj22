**One sentence:** A zombie kid who has never seen the sun walks out under a found helmet, gets caught in a pond at
noon when it breaks, and is let out by the rain his grandpa always called the good weather.

**The premise, in four sentences.** In this town, we went to bed when the sun came up, and nobody would look at it. I
ran out once and came back on fire. Then I found a hat, and I had one day in colour before it broke. On the other
side of the hill was a town with the same rule, the other way round.

## Three hooks, and the one chosen

| | Picture | Words | The question it leaves |
|---|---|---|---|
| A | A dark town under trees, every door shutting as the sky goes pink | "In this town, we went to bed at sunrise." | Why? |
| B | A zombie kid up to his neck in a pond at noon, a broken helmet floating by him | "In this town, the sun could kill me. I was standing in a pond." | How did he get there? |
| **C (chosen)** | A zombie kid at a door open a hand's width. A blade of gold daylight lies across the dark floor. He puts one finger in it and it smokes | "In this town, I had never seen the sun. Nobody had. We went to bed when it came up. I wanted to see it. Just once." | Will he see it, and what will it cost? |

C is chosen because the first frame has the kid, the thing he wants and what it does to him, all in one picture, and
the words are the title. A is an establishing shot with nobody to care about. B starts at the worst moment, but the
wish is what the film runs on, and B spends it.

## What is planted, and where it pays off

| Planted | Pays off |
|---|---|
| Grandpa calls rain "the good weather" and nobody knows why; "I thought he just liked puddles" (2.rain, 2.puddles) | The rain lets him walk out of the pond (7.walk, 7.good); grandpa had been out there too (7.been) |
| The rule: shut the door, and do not look (2.rule, 2.look) | The day town has the same rule about the night (8.door, 8.dark, 8.same) |

| He gives the kid a bone, and the kid keeps it (6.bread, 6.bone) | The kid still has the bone at the meeting place (9.meet) |
| Pink sky is when one town goes in (2.rule) | Pink sky is when the two of them meet (9.meet) |

## The clock

From `5.hat` to `7.broke` a wear bar hangs over the helmet in every shot, and it only goes down. It is green, then
yellow, then red. It is the thing he said the old ones did not tell him.

## Minecraft facts the story stands on

A zombie burns in daylight. One that wears a helmet does not, and the helmet wears out while it keeps the sun off.
A zombie does not burn in water or in the rain. Villagers go to bed at sunset. Sources and what was not looked up:
`Topics/InThisTown/outlines/ideas-2026-11-17.md`.
